# Offscript working principles

Preserve the user's supplied brand and creative intent. Shared design knowledge supports production;
reference examples do not impose a company's identity or voice on projects.

Keep contracts explicit, mechanical validation separate from creative judgment, and approvals and
integrity checks intact. Describe implemented behavior accurately. Reproduce setup and verification
from checked-in source and dependency locks without hidden private inputs or paid inference APIs.

Public source and technical documentation stay focused on current behavior. Product strategy,
roadmap, development research and working ideas remain local in ignored `.local/`.
