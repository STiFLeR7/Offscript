The Argument

Most AI Never Crosses From Pilot to the Work It Was Bought to Do.

Adoption is no longer the problem. Almost every enterprise is already using AI somewhere. The problem is the distance between using it and banking it — between a convincing demo and a system that has changed a number the board watches.

This guide lays out the evidence for that gap, the system we use to close it, the proof of where it pays, and how we hand you a capability you keep. It begins with what the research actually says.

01 · The Evidence

Adoption Has Outrun Value — by a Wide Margin.

Nearly four in five organizations now use AI in at least one function. Yet most report no tangible impact on enterprise-level profit. The tooling arrived; the integration into how the business actually runs did not.

Share of organizations — AI adopted vs. material EBIT impact realized
78% — use AI in at least one business function
<20% — see a material impact on enterprise-level EBIT
Source: McKinsey & Company, The State of AI 2025 (survey of 1,993 respondents). 78% of organizations report using AI in at least one function; more than 80% report no tangible enterprise-level EBIT impact from generative AI. Bars show share of organizations.

The organizations that do see impact have one thing in common: they redesigned the workflow around the AI — they did not bolt it on beside it.
