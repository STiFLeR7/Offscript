# Brand Voice

> **Brand Pack — the WHAT (a swappable brand value).** This is the brand's voice, tone, and
> language with the *intent* behind each rule (Value + Why). It is **lifted verbatim** from the
> source system's `README-COLLATERAL.md` → CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS (the one place voice was authored).
> It is brand-specific by design — when the brand changes, this file is replaced, not the governance.

---

Example Brand copy is **diagnostic, not aspirational**. Headlines indict the reader's status quo, then propose a sharper system.

## Voice

- **Second-person, direct.** "**You** want to scale, but operations are hitting a ceiling." "**You're** paying skilled staff $30–50/hour to do work that costs pennies to automate." "**You Didn't** Hire a Tech Agency to Create New Problems."
- **First-person plural for the company.** "**We** fix this without changing your software." "**We** don't believe in 18-month transformation roadmaps." "**We pair** AI speed with human judgment."
- **Imperative for outcomes.** "Stop Making Decisions Based on 5% of the Data." "Stop Choosing Between Scale & Quality." "Don't just hire more people. Hire Digital Workers."

## Tone

- **Blunt and quantified.** Almost every body block carries a number: 40% capacity reclamation, $2.16M in capital leaks, 92% lower cost, 3× reply-rate improvement, 4 weeks to deployment, 25+ hrs saved per rep per week.
- **Diagnostic / problem-first.** Pages open with a *named tax* on the reader: "The Labor Tax", "The Error Cost", "The Opportunity Cost", "The Middleman Tax", "The Bill You Can't Decode". Solution comes second.
- **Plain English over jargon.** "Manual glue." "Digital workers." "Messy data." "Workflow that bleeds money." Where jargon appears (APA, ROI, CRM), it's spelled out the first time.
- **Quiet on hype.** No exclamation points. No emoji. No rocket ships. The phrase "agent" appears constantly; the phrase "AI-powered" almost never does.

## Casing

- **Title Case for headlines and section titles** ("Build Your Personalized Sales Outreach At Scale", "How Example Brand Executes This For You"). Sentence-cased fragments only inside body / lists.
- **CAPS reserved** for the eyebrow micro-label ("HOW IT WORKS", "ENTERPRISE SECURITY & COMPLIANCE") and never for body.
- **Inline emphasis** uses **bold** mid-sentence on the operative noun ("an **autonomous AI agent**", "delivering measurable **ROI within weeks**", "shift from sampling to **agentic intelligence**"). No italics for emphasis; italics are for product names rarely.

## I vs. you

| Speaker | Pronoun | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| The reader / customer | **you / your** | Pain points, current state, costs, outcomes the reader will see |
| Example Brand | **we / our** | What Example Brand does, the methodology, the promise |

Brand never says "I." Headlines avoid "we" — the reader stays the subject ("Your Tech Ownership Without Oversight" — not "Our Tech Ownership Service").

## Emoji & icons

**No emoji, ever.** Iconography is small, monoline, seated inside a circular soft-clay periwinkle chip (glyph in the periwinkle accent `#4F57C4`) — never a bare icon next to copy. Decorative ornament is geometric (navy clips, tonal bands), never illustrative.

## Concrete examples

- **Headline pattern:** *"Turn Your Customer Conversations into Competitive Advantage"* — verb + customer's noun + into + outcome.
- **Sub-headline pattern:** *"An autonomous AI agent that audits every customer interaction, identifies revenue opportunities, eliminates compliance risks, and replicates your top performers — delivering measurable ROI within weeks."* — single sentence, four parallel verbs, dash-clause for the proof.
- **CTA copy:** Always 2 short words. "Let's Talk", "Book Your 1:1 Call →", "Request a Demo", "Stop Making Decisions Based on 5% of the Data" pairs with an inline `Let's Talk` button.
- **Closing pattern:** Every brochure ends with a dark-navy CTA strip. Eyebrow indicts ("Stop Choosing Between Scale & Quality."), supportive line softens ("Break the Traditional Sales Law. Don't Compromise."), button is electric blue.
