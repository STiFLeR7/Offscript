Flywheel · a reinforcing cycle — each stage drives the next (Level 3, max 6 stages). Governing rules: the Diagrams Rulebook (README-DIAGRAMS.md — §8 flywheel, §4 rendering, §12 style). SVG is permitted (the circular arrangement and curved between-stage arrows can't be expressed in flow/grid). Stage nodes sit on a ring at even angles; between each consecutive pair a short arc (A command) carries a <marker> arrowhead clockwise. Flat tonal nodes, hairline arrows — no gradients. On navy a scoped override lifts the arrows to stone and the centre label to white. Inline SVG + CSS, no JavaScript. Doc-scoped (never in colors_and_type.css). Illustrative stages.
