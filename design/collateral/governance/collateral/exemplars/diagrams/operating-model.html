1 · Three stacked layers · Inputs ↓ Capabilities ↓ Outputs, centred downward flow
Layer 01
Inputs
Signals
Tickets, documents and events captured from the business.
Context
Policies, records and prior decisions retrieved on demand.
Intent
The outcome a requester or operator is asking for.
Layer 02
Capabilities
Reason
Agents plan, retrieve and decide against the policy.
Act
Tools and integrations execute the chosen steps.
Supervise
A human reviews exceptions and approves escalations.
Layer 03
Outputs
Resolutions
Cases closed and records written back to the system.
Handoffs
Edge cases routed to the right person with context.
Evidence
An audit trail of every decision and action taken.

Operating Model · how a system operates, Inputs → Capabilities → Outputs (Level 2). Governing rules: the Diagrams Rulebook (README-DIAGRAMS.md — §8 operating model, §6 layout intelligence, §10 nodes & connectors, §12 style). The orientation is fixed by the family: vertical, a maximum of 3 major layers — never a grid, never a fourth band. Each layer is a full-width row — a left layer-label rail + a horizontal set of item nodes; a single centred downward connector between layers carries the flow, relationship only, no ornament. Nodes take a soft-clay .tone-* surface; no gradients. Pure HTML/CSS — Flexbox/Grid, no JavaScript, no SVG, no absolute positioning. Doc-scoped (never in colors_and_type.css). Illustrative agentic operations model.
