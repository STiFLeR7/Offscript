## What belongs here (the HOW — brand-invariant)

For **each** component, three things and nothing pixel-level:
- **Job** — what it communicates / the role it plays.
- **When to reach for it** — the use case that selects it.
- **Rules** — the constraints it must obey (expressed brand-invariantly).
