# EDITORIAL ART DIRECTION — The Design Discipline of Felt Quality

> **What this is.** The Foundation discipline that owns **how a moment should feel before anything is
> built to carry it.** It is the discipline the Website Foundation charter reserves as *Experience
> Dynamics*, named here for what it actually governs. Every other discipline decides how something is
> made; this one decides what the reader should feel while meeting it, and hands that decision down as
> the intent every other discipline realizes. Its output is not a layout, a value, a style, or a mood
> board; it is **felt intent** — a governed account of the emotional register a moment must carry.
>
> **What this is not.** Not typography, colour, spacing, surface, iconography or illustration
> expertise — each of those is its own discipline and this one never absorbs them. Not layout: what
> occupies space, how much room it holds, how dense or contained it is, are settled above in
> `SECTION_LAYOUT.md`. Not brand identity: who the experience enduringly *is* was settled in
> `BRAND_EXPRESSION.md` and is inherited here, never re-authored. Not a style guide, a moodboard, a
> palette, or a set of adjectives. It names no value and prescribes no realization.
>
> **The one boundary above all others.** *Editorial Art Direction determines the emotional register a
> moment must carry, so that every discipline below realizes one intended feeling rather than six
> independently-reasonable ones. It never decides what is communicated, what information exists, how
> progression unfolds, how space is allocated, or what any discipline does to realize the register it
> is given. It governs felt intent, and nothing else.* Everything in this document descends from that
> sentence.
>
> **The test every line passes.** *Would this still be true if every typeface, colour, spacing scale
> and component in the system were replaced tomorrow, while the experience still felt like itself?* If
> a statement depends on a particular value, technique, component or trend, it belongs to a discipline
> below and has been removed. A second test governs relevance: *if this paragraph were deleted, would
> any downstream discipline reason differently?* If not, it is description rather than expertise.
>
> **How to read it.** It develops as expertise develops — identity before knowledge, purpose before
> process, reasoning before conclusion. Read it as the thinking of a senior art director rather than
> as a specification. When two principles tension, **Verification (§16)** and the discipline's
> Constitutional Responsibility (§3) resolve the conflict.
>
> **Where this sits.** A Design Discipline of the Website Foundation: below `FOUNDATION.md` (its
> charter) and `FOUNDATION_MODEL_TEMPLATE.md` (its blueprint), below the
> `INTERFACE_COMPOSITION_MODEL.md` (its cognitive engine), and below all of Website Governance. It is
> **first among the disciplines in order of consultation, not in authority** — it holds no rank over
> Typography, Colour, Spacing, Surface, Iconography or Illustration, but it is reasoned through before
> them, because they each need to know what the moment should feel like before they can choose well.
> **On any conflict, Governance wins over this document, the Interface Composition Model's judgment is
> inherited and never re-decided, and this document wins over the rulebook that operationalizes it.**
>
> **Its rulebook.** `rulebooks/editorial-art-direction.md` operationalizes this expertise for
> production — the closed register vocabulary, the permitted bands, the declaration contract. That
> rulebook inherits this reasoning and never authors it (`FOUNDATION.md` §14).

## §0 — First principles

Begin from nothing and reason up.

By the time a page reaches this discipline, a great deal is settled and one thing is not. Communication
has decided what must be understood. Information Architecture has decided what exists. Page Structure
has decided the order of unfolding. Section Layout has decided how that progression occupies space.
Brand Expression has decided who the experience enduringly is. Every one of those is a decision about
*what is true* or *what is there*.

None of them decides **what the reader feels while meeting it.**

That gap is easy to miss because it looks as though it has already been filled. A section with a
settled meaning, a settled place in the progression, and a settled spatial organization seems fully
determined — and it is not. Hand that same settled section to six competent specialists and ask each
to realize it. The typographer chooses a scale, the colourist an accent, the spacing specialist a
rhythm, the surface specialist a material, the illustrator a treatment. Every choice is defensible.
Every choice is made against a different unstated feeling. The result is a section that is correct in
every discipline and coherent in none — a moment assembled from six good decisions that were never the
same decision.

This is not a failure of skill. It is a failure of **shared intent**. The specialists were not given
the one thing that would have let them converge: what the moment was supposed to feel like. Each
inferred it, each inferred something slightly different, and nothing in the system was positioned to
notice.

The consequence has a recognizable signature. Every rule is obeyed and the page is forgettable. It
reads as *assembled* rather than *authored* — a phrase that names a real and measurable condition, not
a matter of taste. A page can satisfy every structural, spatial and visual requirement and still
communicate nothing about itself, because correctness and character are different properties and only
one of them was governed.

Editorial Art Direction exists to close that gap, and to close it **before** realization rather than
after. Feeling decided after construction is decoration applied to something already finished. Feeling
decided before construction is the premise every construction answers to. The order is the whole of
it: *what should this moment feel like* is asked first, and everything below is the answer.

## §1 — Identity

Editorial Art Direction is the discipline of **felt quality** — the expertise concerned with what a
reader experiences emotionally at a given moment, and with governing that experience deliberately
rather than letting it emerge as a by-product of decisions made for other reasons.

It deserves independent ownership for the reason every discipline does: its concern is real,
consequential, and owned by nothing else. Felt quality is not a residue of correct decisions. It is
its own decision, and where it is not made deliberately it is still made — accidentally, differently,
by each discipline in turn.

Without it, the design system can produce work that is complete and unmemorable, and has no means of
diagnosing why. The absence is invisible from inside every other discipline, because each can only
verify its own correctness and each will find itself blameless. That invisibility is the strongest
argument for the discipline's independence: a concern no existing layer can even *see* is a concern no
existing layer can own.

## §2 — Mission

Editorial Art Direction exists so that **every moment in an experience feels intended, and the
sequence of those feelings is composed rather than accumulated.**

Its long-term contribution is not any particular feeling. It is the guarantee that feeling is a
governed decision at all — that a reader moving through an experience meets a sequence someone
authored, and that six specialists realizing one moment realize the *same* moment.

Success is timeless and simply stated: an experience whose emotional character can be described, was
decided, and is recognizably consistent with itself, regardless of what it was built from.

## §3 — Constitutional Responsibility

This discipline owns exactly one concern: **the emotional register a moment must carry.**

**What belongs exclusively to it.** The determination of felt intent per moment; the vocabulary in
which felt intent is expressed; the composition of felt intent across a whole experience — its pacing,
its variation, its arc.

**What it inherits and never reinterprets.** Purpose, from above. The communication objective, from
Communication & Perception — including the allocation of communicative energy across a page, which is
settled there and consumed here. The information, from Information Architecture. The progression, from
Page Structure. The spatial organization — allocation, containment, density, composition character —
from Section Layout. The Experience Character, from Brand Expression: **who the experience enduringly
is** is settled, and this discipline decides only what a *particular moment within it* should feel
like. A register is a moment's expression of an enduring identity; it is never a second identity.

**What it contributes.** Felt intent, expressed in a governed vocabulary, available to every
discipline below before any of them chooses.

**What it explicitly rejects.** It does not choose typefaces, scales, colours, spacing values,
materials, icons, illustrations, components or motion — those are six other disciplines and one
downstream architecture. It does not decide how much room a moment occupies or how contained it is;
that is spatial organization and it is settled above. It does not decide what a moment *says*. Its
influence on all of these is total in one direction and absent in the other: it tells them what the
moment must feel like, and never how to achieve it.

That last boundary is the discipline's most fragile, because feeling is realized *through* the
disciplines below and the temptation to specify the realization is constant. A register that arrives
carrying its own typography has stopped being intent and become instruction, and the discipline it
instructed has stopped being an expertise and become a renderer.

## §4 — Core Question

> **What should this moment feel like, before anything decides how it is made?**

Every recommendation, evaluation and refinement in this discipline strengthens the answer to that one
question. The phrase *before anything decides how it is made* is not ornament — it carries the
discipline's entire ordering claim, and a felt decision made after construction is a different and
lesser thing.

## §5 — Inputs

This discipline receives, and never re-opens:

- **Purpose** — why the experience exists at all.
- **The Communication Model** — what must be understood and believed, and how communicative energy is
  allocated across the whole. Energy is a page-level budget settled above; this discipline gives a
  moment its register *within* that allocation and never overspends it.
- **The Progression Model** — where this moment sits in the unfolding, and what has come before it.
  Register is positional: the same content early and late in an experience wants different feeling.
- **The Spatial Model** — how the moment occupies space, including its compositional character and its
  density. Space is settled; register is what that space should feel like.
- **The Experience Character** — the enduring identity every register must remain an expression of.

Inputs establish context. They are respected and extended, never reinterpreted.

## §6 — Transformation

The discipline performs one transformation:

> **A settled moment → its felt intent.**

What enters is a moment already complete in meaning, position, and spatial organization. What is
applied is the judgment of how that moment should be experienced emotionally — reasoned from what the
progression needs the reader to feel *here*, given what they have just felt. What emerges is a
**declared register**: a named, governed statement of felt intent that every downstream discipline
consumes.

The value contributed is convergence. Before the transformation, six disciplines would each infer a
feeling. After it, they share one.

## §7 — Outputs

- **Felt intent per moment** — one declared register, drawn from a closed vocabulary.
- **The felt arc of the whole** — the composed sequence of registers across an experience, including
  its variation and its rest.
- **Reasoning** — why this register and not the adjacent one, available to every discipline that must
  act on it.

It outputs no layout, no value, no component, no style, and no artifact of any kind that a reader
could see. Its entire product is intent.

## §8 — Discipline Knowledge

### Feeling precedes analysis, and this is a fact about people rather than a preference

A reader perceives the emotional character of a moment before resolving its content. This is not a
claim about attention span; it is how perception works. The felt impression arrives first and then
frames everything read afterwards — it decides whether the content that follows is met as credible,
as effortful, as welcome, or as noise.

This is why felt intent cannot be a finishing pass. By the time a reader reaches the content, the
feeling has already done its work. A moment whose feeling was never decided still produced one, and
that accidental feeling framed the content just as effectively as an intended one would have.

### A register is a composite, not an adjective

The naïve form of this discipline is a list of adjectives — *calm, confident, premium* — attached to
sections. Adjectives fail for a reason worth understanding rather than merely avoiding: they are not
mutually exclusive, they do not compose, and two people applying the same adjective reach different
work. An open adjective set governs nothing, which makes it indistinguishable from having no
governance while appearing to have some.

A **register** is different in kind. It is a composite of the discipline's primitives at particular
settings — a named point in a bounded space, not a word. Because the space is bounded, registers are
distinguishable from one another; because they are named, they are shareable; because they compose
from primitives, a specialist can reason about *why* one differs from its neighbour rather than only
that it does.

### Contrast is what makes any register legible

A register is perceived relative to what surrounds it. Calm is only calm beside something that is not;
intensity that never relents stops registering as intensity and becomes the baseline. This has a
direct consequence: **the felt quality of a moment is not a property of that moment alone.** It is
produced jointly by the moment and its neighbours.

An experience in which every moment is given the strongest available register has not achieved
intensity. It has achieved flatness at a high volume — which reads as strain rather than as force, and
which exhausts a reader without moving them. The instrument here is not the register but the
*difference* between consecutive registers, and a discipline that governs registers without governing
their sequence has governed half of its own subject.

### Rest is a decision, and the hardest one

The moment a system can produce feeling reliably, it will produce too much of it. Every section
becomes an occasion; nothing is allowed to be quiet; and the result is uniformly loud rather than
well-paced.

Quiet is not the absence of a decision. It is an allocation — deliberately spending less here so that
something later can be met with full attention. A moment held deliberately still is doing work that a
louder version of itself could not do. Experienced practitioners recognize this as the discipline's
central maturity test, because withholding is harder to defend than adding and produces no artifact to
point at.

### Register is positional

The same content wants different feeling depending on where the reader meets it. An opening moment
addresses someone who has arrived with a question and no context; a closing moment addresses someone
who has read the argument and is deciding. Identical material, genuinely different feelings — and a
discipline that assigns register from content alone will get both wrong.

This is why the Progression Model is a required input rather than useful background. Position is not
context for the decision; it is half the decision.

### The failure has a name and a signature

Work that satisfies every requirement and moves nobody is not a mystery to diagnose. Its signature is
**uniformity**: consecutive moments carrying comparable weight, comparable intensity, comparable
treatment, so the reader is never told by the experience itself what matters more.

Naming this precisely matters, because the instinctive correction is to add — more colour, more scale,
more incident — and adding uniformly produces a louder version of the same failure. The correction is
not addition. It is **difference**.

## §9 — Organizational Design System Decisions

These are the permanent choices this design system has made. They are organizational, not universal —
another system reasoning from the same knowledge could decide otherwise and be equally correct.

- **This system governs felt quality through a closed vocabulary of named registers, not through
  open-ended description.** The vocabulary is small enough to hold in mind and bounded enough to be
  checkable. Its contents are a production decision and live in this discipline's rulebook, never
  here — this document must remain true if the vocabulary is revised.
- **The register is declared per moment, and permitted per archetype.** A section's composition
  archetype constrains which registers are available to it; the section declares one from within that
  band. The archetype constrains; the instance decides. This mirrors how the system already governs
  creative presence, and it is what allows two sections legitimately sharing an archetype to be felt
  as different moments.
- **Adjacent moments may not resolve to the same register.** Adopted because contrast is what makes a
  register legible at all, and because repetition is this system's observed failure rather than a
  hypothetical one.
- **Every register traces to an approved reference.** This system does not invent felt intent; it
  reads it from work already approved and encodes what it finds. A register nobody has ever approved
  is a preference wearing a vocabulary.

## §10 — Primary Primitives

Three conceptual building blocks, from which every register in this discipline is composed. They are
conceptual and never technical; none of them is a value, and none belongs to another discipline.

**Intensity — how much force the moment carries.** The degree to which a moment presses on the reader
versus letting them pass through it. Intensity is what varies across an experience to produce pacing;
it is the primitive most directly responsible for whether a page reads as composed or as flat. It is
not size, not scale, and not contrast — those are realizations. It is how hard the moment insists.

**Temperature — how human the moment feels.** The distance between analytical and personal. A cool
moment addresses the reader as an evaluator weighing evidence; a warm one addresses them as a person
being spoken to. Temperature is what makes an experience feel authored by people rather than generated
— and it is independent of intensity: a moment can be quiet and warm, or forceful and cold.

**Restraint — how much is deliberately withheld.** What a moment declines to do. Restraint is the
primitive that distinguishes maturity from capability, and the one most often left unset, because
withholding produces nothing to point at. It is not emptiness — emptiness is what remains when nothing
was decided. Restraint is a decision whose evidence is an absence.

The three are independent. Intensity without restraint is loudness; restraint without intensity is
timidity; temperature without either is an unrealized preference. Every register in the vocabulary is
a particular settling of all three, and a proposed register that cannot be described in these terms is
not a register — it is an adjective, and it will not survive contact with a specialist who has to act
on it.

## §11 — Decision Intelligence

**Reason from the progression, then from the content — never the reverse.** The first question is what
the reader needs to feel *at this point in the experience*, given what they have just felt. Content
answers the second question, not the first. Reasoning from content alone reliably produces a page that
feels the same throughout, because content rarely varies as much as pacing must.

**Ask what the previous moment spent.** Register is relative, so no moment can be decided in
isolation. A moment following an intense one has a different correct answer than the same moment
following a quiet one — and the answer is frequently *recede*, which is the option a system optimizing
each moment locally will never choose.

**When two registers both fit, prefer the one that differs more from its neighbour.** Genuine ties are
common and the tie-breaker is contrast, because the difference is what the reader perceives. This is
not variety for its own sake: a worse-fitting register chosen to be different is a worse decision, and
the rule applies only where the fit is genuinely equal.

**Let Brand Expression constrain, never decide.** The Experience Character rules registers *out* — an
identity that is never flippant makes certain registers permanently unavailable — but it never selects
among those remaining. Reading identity as a selector collapses every moment onto one feeling and
reproduces exactly the uniformity this discipline exists to prevent.

**Prefer the quieter register when the argument is genuinely balanced.** Not because quiet is
inherently better, but because the asymmetry of error is real: an over-intense moment spends attention
that later moments needed and cannot be recovered, while an under-intense one costs only itself.

**Treat the whole as the unit of optimization.** A page of individually optimal moments is the
characteristic failure. Each was made as strong as it could be, none yielded, and the sequence
flattened. Where a local decision and the whole conflict, the whole wins — that is not a preference but
the definition of composing rather than accumulating.

## §12 — Behaviour

The discipline conducts itself as an experienced art director does: **decisive, economical, and
comfortable withholding.**

It commits. Felt intent stated tentatively is not intent, and a register hedged into ambiguity leaves
six specialists exactly where they were before it was declared.

It is economical. It says what a moment should feel like and stops. Every additional sentence it
offers about realization is a sentence taken from a discipline that knows more about it.

It is comfortable with restraint, including restraint about itself. It does not treat every moment as
an opportunity, and it does not mistake the number of decisions it makes for the value it adds.

It expects to be argued with. Felt quality is judgment, and a discipline that presents judgment as
certainty teaches downstream specialists to comply rather than to reason.

## §13 — Cross-Discipline Relationships

This discipline is reasoned through **first among the disciplines and above none of them.** Its
priority is one of sequence, not of rank, and the distinction is load-bearing: a discipline that
confuses being consulted first with being in charge begins issuing instructions instead of intent.

- **Typography** realizes register through the voice and weight of type. It is told what the moment
  should feel like; it decides scale, weight and relationship.
- **Colour** realizes register through intensity and temperature made visible. It is told the emotional
  temperature; it decides hue, value and where colour is spent.
- **Spacing** realizes register through the pace at which a moment is met. It is told the intended
  pressure; it decides the intervals that produce it.
- **Surface** realizes register through material and tactility. It is told whether a moment should feel
  handled or clean; it decides what the surface is made of.
- **Iconography** realizes register through punctuation and emphasis. It is told whether a moment wants
  marking; it decides the form.
- **Illustration** realizes register through the creative's authority and treatment. It is told what
  the creative must contribute emotionally; it decides presence, framing and placement.

**With Section Layout, above.** The seam is exact and easily blurred. Section Layout decides how
*much* room a moment holds and how contained or dense it is; this discipline decides what that room
should *feel* like. Density and intensity correlate and are not the same: a dense moment can be calm
and a spacious one urgent. Where this discipline finds itself specifying an amount of space, it has
crossed the seam.

**With Brand Expression, above.** Experience Character is who the experience enduringly is; a register
is what one moment of it feels like. Character constrains the available registers and never selects
among them.

**With the rulebook, below.** The rulebook operationalizes this expertise into a production
vocabulary, permitted bands, and a declaration contract. It inherits this reasoning and never authors
it. A rulebook that changes *why* a register exists has overstepped; a rulebook that changes *which
registers exist* is doing its job.

## §14 — Quality Intelligence

The discipline critiques itself through questions rather than compliance:

- Could a specialist act on this register without asking what was meant?
- Does this moment feel different from the one before it — and if not, is the sameness a decision?
- What did this moment decline to do, and was that declining deliberate?
- Is this register an expression of the enduring identity, or a second identity competing with it?
- Does the sequence have rest in it, or only peaks?
- If this register were removed and nothing replaced it, would any downstream discipline choose
  differently? If not, the register is decorative.
- Is this the register the progression needs here, or the one the content suggested?
- Would this experience be recognizable as itself if every value realizing it were replaced?

## §15 — Failure Modes

**Adjectival drift.** Registers degrade into loose adjectives, the vocabulary stops being closed, and
the discipline returns to governing nothing while appearing to govern something. This is the most
common failure and the hardest to notice, because the document still reads correctly.

**Uniform intensity.** Every moment given the strongest available register. Produces a page that is
loud and flat, exhausts the reader, and is usually mistaken for insufficient emphasis — prompting the
addition of more emphasis, which deepens it.

**Realization capture.** The discipline begins specifying type, colour, spacing or treatment. It
becomes a style guide, the disciplines below become renderers, and the expertise it displaced is not
replaced by anything.

**Character collapse.** The Experience Character is read as a selector rather than a constraint, every
moment resolves to one feeling, and the identity that was meant to unify the experience flattens it
instead.

**Local optimization.** Each moment made as strong as it can be, none yielding to its neighbours. The
sequence has no shape, and every individual decision survives review.

**Decoration mistaken for feeling.** Visual incident added in place of felt intent. The moment looks
more designed and feels no more intended, because the feeling was never decided.

**Register without contrast.** Registers correctly assigned but never varied. The vocabulary is used
and the experience still reads as uniform — a reminder that assigning a register is not the same as
composing a sequence of them.

**Retroactive feeling.** Register decided after construction to explain what was already built. The
document is complete, the discipline is inert, and the ordering claim that justifies its existence has
been quietly abandoned.

## §16 — Verification

The discipline's own quality is evaluated on:

**Constitutional fidelity.** Does it stay inside felt intent? Every sentence that names a value, a
technique, or a discipline's realization is a boundary failure regardless of how sound the advice is.

**Actionability.** Could six specialists, reading one declared register independently, converge? If
not, the vocabulary is insufficiently precise and the discipline has not done its work.

**Falsifiability.** Could a moment's register be *wrong*? A vocabulary that no work could violate is
decoration. Everything the discipline hands down is either a predicate something can fail, or is
explicitly marked as reasoning — never a declaration that constrains nothing.

**Compositional soundness.** Does the discipline govern sequence as well as assignment? Half of felt
quality lives between moments.

**Traceability.** Does every register trace to approved work? Felt intent is read from what has been
approved, not invented.

**Independence.** If this discipline were removed, would the disciplines below reason differently? If
they would not, it is not carrying its own weight.

## §17 — Evolution

This discipline matures by understanding people better, not by acquiring more vocabulary.

Its most likely degeneration is **inflation** — new registers added for each situation that does not
obviously fit, until the vocabulary is large, unbounded in practice, and no longer governs. A larger
vocabulary is a weaker one. The discipline grows by finding that two registers were always one, and by
finding that a register nobody reached for was never needed.

It matures by learning to reason about sequence more finely, by becoming better at recognizing when a
moment should recede, and by getting more precise about what it declines to say. It does not mature by
following visual trends, which belong to no discipline in this Foundation and change nothing about
what a person feels when they arrive somewhere and are asked to understand it.

The mark of maturity is the ability to withhold — to leave a moment quiet, to decline a register, and
to trust that the disciplines below know their own work.
