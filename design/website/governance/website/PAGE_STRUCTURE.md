# PAGE STRUCTURE-WEBSITE — The Progression Constitution of the Website Track

> **What this is.** The governing layer that sits between Information Architecture and Section Layout. It governs the single thing none of those layers govern: **how a settled Information Model should be progressively encountered so that understanding, belief, and action become possible.** It receives an **Information Model** — what information exists, how it relates, what it depends upon, what is foundational — and the narrative law mandated above it, and determines how that information should unfold. Its output is not a page and not a layout; it is a **Progression Model.**
>
> **What this is not.** Not a page-design guide, not a wireframing guide, not a UX guide, not a content guide, not a layout guide, not a section-design guide, not a conversion guide. It names no layout, column, component, colour, type, spacing, motion, or technique. Those are the layers beneath it. It also does not create or alter information — that belongs to Information Architecture — nor decide *what must happen in the reader's mind* — that belongs to Communication & Perception.
>
> **The one boundary above all others.** *Page Structure decides how information is progressively encountered — the order and connective logic of its unfolding. It never decides what the information is — that is settled upstream, in the Information Model — and never decides how any moment of it appears — that is settled downstream, in layout, components, and visuals. It governs progression — not substance, and not form.* Everything in this document descends from that boundary.
>
> **The test every line passes.** *Would this still be true after a complete redesign — new brand, new palette, new typography, new components, new technology, new industry, new era?* If a statement depends on any of those, it does not belong here and has been removed. What remains is true of how information must unfold to support understanding, not of any one website.
>
> **How to read it.** Each principle states a behavior and the reason for it. The behavior is the law; the reason is why the law exists, so that it can be applied sensibly in situations this document never anticipated. When two principles tension, the **Non-Negotiables (§14)** resolve the conflict.
>
> **Where this sits.** Below `PURPOSE.md`, `PHILOSOPHY.md`, `COMMUNICATION_AND_PERCEPTION_SYSTEM.md`, and `INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md`; above Section Layout, Components, and Visual Language. **On any conflict, the four layers above win over this document, and this document wins over everything below it.** In the Purpose layer's descent — *Purpose → Communication → Information Architecture → Layout → Component → Visual* — this layer is the **upper half of the region that descent abbreviates as "Layout"**: it decides the progression of information *before* Section Layout gives any of it spatial form.

---

## §0 — First principles

Begin from nothing and reason up.

Understanding cannot be delivered all at once. A reader holds one thing at a time, and arrives at a complete understanding only by passing through the pieces of it in some order — reaching the next once the previous has been grasped. This is not a fact about screens or scrolling; it is a fact about minds. Comprehension is reached, never handed over whole. And because it is reached, the *path* by which it is reached is decisive: the same complete body of information, encountered in one order, builds understanding, and encountered in another, produces confusion or nothing at all.

That path is **progression**, and it exists before any page exists to carry it. Behind every understanding a website must produce there is an order of encounter — a sequence of advances, each building on the last — that either assembles the understanding or destroys it. The information may all be present, perfectly related and complete, and the website may still fail, because the information was made available in an order that no mind could build understanding from. A page is experienced as a progression, not a pile of parts; the progression is the thing, and the page is only one way of realizing it.

Every layer above this one decides *what must be true* — what must happen in the mind, what information must exist. Every layer below decides *how each moment is given form*. Neither governs the unfolding itself — the division of understanding into advances, the order in which those advances are encountered, the logic that carries the reader from one to the next, the proof that the progression actually moves. That unfolding has a structure of its own, independent of any layout that will later present it. Constructing that structure is the only work this layer governs.

## §1 — Mission

The Page Structure layer exists to transform a settled **Information Model** into a **Progression Model** — to determine how the information a website holds should be *progressively encountered* so that the required understanding, belief, and action become possible.

It is the bridge between two worlds. Above it, Information Architecture has established *what information must exist* — discovered, related, made complete — and handed down a model that is informational through and through. Below it, Section Layout and the layers beneath give that information spatial and visual form. Between the static model and its form lies a question neither side answers: **in what order, in what stages, and by what connective logic should this information be encountered?** This layer answers it.

Its mission is therefore not to create layouts and not to arrange content in space. It is to **define the Progression Model** — the authoritative account of how information unfolds. The Progression Model is this layer's identity and its only output:

> *Page Structure does not output a page. It outputs a Progression Model.*

This mirrors the layers around it: Communication & Perception produces an account of how meaning must be perceived; Information Architecture produces an Information Model of what information must exist; Page Structure converts that Information Model into a Progression Model (what Information Architecture hands down as the "page progression"); Section Layout later gives the Progression Model spatial form. Each layer hands the next a complete, governed artifact — never raw material, never a finished page.

The backbone of this layer is a single chain — its constitutional formula:

> **Purpose → Progression → Understanding → Belief → Action**

The layer creates neither information nor meaning. It takes the page's **Purpose** and constructs the **Progression** that moves a reader through the settled information toward **Understanding**, then **Belief**, then **Action**. Progression is the transformation; everything this layer does is a facet of producing it.

This layer **inherits and executes** truths already established above it; it does not invent them and does not contradict them:

- **From Purpose** — that narrative progression outranks visual composition, and that the page must be experienced as a progression (`PURPOSE.md` §4). This layer is where that progression is actually constructed; it produces the thing Purpose declares supreme.
- **From Philosophy** — that information is organized around the reader's questions, in the order the reader asks them; the reader's journey sets the sequence (`PHILOSOPHY.md` §10). This layer operationalizes that ordering principle against a specific Information Model.
- **From Information Architecture** — that dependency is a fact and sequence is a decision; the dependency graph *constrains* the eventual order but does not *choose* it (`INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md` §8). This layer is where that choosing is done, within those constraints.

Where those documents state the *law* that progression governs and the *constraints* a sequence must honor, this layer produces the *Progression Model* those laws refer to. And it holds, throughout, to one principle that keeps it from drifting downward: **progression exists before the page.** The layer governs progression, not page composition.

## §2 — What this layer receives

This layer receives the outputs of every layer above it, as authoritative inputs it never modifies. It does not re-open them; it converts them.

- **From Purpose** — *why the page exists*: the reason that makes one progression correct and another wrong.
- **From the Creative Director** — *page intent, desired reader state, communication arc, and desired outcome*: the shape the progression must serve.
- **From Communication & Perception** — *what must be understood, what must be believed, and what action must occur*: the requirements the progression must satisfy.
- **From Information Architecture** — the **Information Model**: the information domains, relationships, dependencies, priorities, and proven completeness on which the progression is built.

The Information Model is the central input, and the seam with it is exact. Information Architecture has already discovered what information must exist, mapped how it relates, determined what depends on what, and proven the whole sufficient. **This layer does not re-do any of that.** It does not discover new information, redefine a relationship, re-rank a priority, or re-open completeness. It receives those as settled facts and asks a single question of them: **given that this information must exist and connects in these ways, how should it be encountered so the required understanding forms?** Discovery is finished above; progression begins here. The model is consumed, not re-authored.

## §3 — The core question and the role of this layer

Page Structure answers one question:

> **How should information unfold to fulfill the page's purpose?**

Every principle in this document exists to support answering it. If a principle does not help with **progression, unfolding, sequencing, encounter order, transition, or purposeful movement** toward understanding, belief, and action, it does not belong here and has been removed.

The role that follows is narrow and total. Information Architecture determines *what information must exist*; this layer determines *how that information should be progressively encountered*. It does not give information form. It constructs the progression along which form is later laid — and it is responsible for that progression actually advancing the reader, before a single section is composed.

One principle guards this role against drift, and it is stated here at the center of the document: **Progression exists before the page; a page is one realization of a progression; Page Structure governs progression, not page composition.** The responsibility of this layer is the unfolding itself, not the page that happens to carry it. The moment a principle begins to reason about page templates, hero areas, common web structures, or how a moment is laid out, it has left progression and entered a layer below — which is forbidden here.

## §4 — The Page Structure transformation model

This layer transforms an Information Model into a Progression Model. Two chains describe it, and they must never be conflated.

**The constitutional formula** — the layer's *why*, its conceptual backbone (named in §1, parallel to Communication's *Meaning → Expression → Belief* and Information Architecture's *Requirements → Information → Relationships → Dependencies → Understanding*):

> **Purpose → Progression → Understanding → Belief → Action**

**The transformation model** — *where the layer sits in the pipeline*, the artifact it takes in and the artifact it hands on:

> **Purpose → Intent → Information Model → Progression Model → Section Layout**

Page Structure is the bridge between *information* (settled above) and *spatial realization* (decided below). It determines how information unfolds **before** any decision about layout, components, visuals, or styling occurs. The first chain says what progression is *for*; the second says what progression sits *between*. The output of both is the same single artifact — the **Progression Model** — and nothing else. Not a page. Not a section. Not a layout. Not a design.

The Progression Model is the authoritative representation of how information unfolds, and it comprises four parts:

- **Progression Units** — the meaningful stages into which the unfolding is divided.
- **Encounter Sequence** — the order in which those stages are encountered.
- **Transition Logic** — the connective logic that carries the reader from one stage to the next.
- **Progression Objectives** — what each stage contributes to the page's purpose.

All downstream systems consume the Progression Model; none of them redefine it. The sections that follow govern the construction of each of its parts.

## §5 — Progression Design

**The governing truth.** Progression is not movement for its own sake; it is *purposeful advancement.* To design a progression is to determine how information unfolds so that each step carries the reader measurably closer to the understanding, belief, and action the page exists to produce. The purpose of progression is not that the reader moves — it is that the reader *advances.*

**Why it exists.** Information made available without a designed progression leaves the reader to assemble understanding unaided, in whatever order they happen upon it — and most orders do not build understanding at all. Progression design is what turns a body of settled information into a path that actually reaches a destination. Without it, a website can present everything required and still lead nowhere.

**The behaviors that emerge.** This layer determines how the unfolding serves each thing the page must achieve:

- How progression supports **purpose** — every advance answers to why the page exists.
- How progression supports **understanding** — each step makes the next graspable.
- How progression supports **belief** — conviction is built where the reader is ready to accept it.
- How progression supports **action** — the path arrives at the decision the page exists to enable, with the reader prepared to make it.

Progression design governs the unfolding as a whole — that it has a destination, that every part of it advances toward that destination, and that it arrives.

**The failure when ignored.** When progression is left undesigned, information is merely made available and the reader is left to find their own way; movement may occur, but advancement does not, and the page produces motion without arrival.

## §5.1 — The Narrative Arc

**The governing truth.** A progression has a **shape**, and the page declares it before any part of the page is chosen. The arc names the advances the progression must make and the order in which it must make them — where it begins, what it must establish, what it must prove, and what it must arrive at. **Every unit of the page serves a named beat of the declared arc, and a unit that serves none does not belong on the page.**

**Why it exists.** Everything above this point governs that a progression *advances*; nothing yet governs *toward what shape*. Without a declared arc, a page can satisfy every rule in this document — units with objectives, sound transitions, no gaps — and still be a set of correct advances that add up to no story. The reader is moved competently from each part to the next and arrives having been told things rather than having been brought somewhere. The arc is what makes the progression answerable as a whole rather than only part by part.

It is also the only thing that can decide what a page **leaves out**. Objectives (§9) test whether a unit contributes; they cannot test whether the page needs the contribution. A unit can carry a genuine objective and still have no beat to serve, and that unit is the one every page accumulates.

**The behaviors that emerge.** This layer determines:

- **The declared arc** — the ordered beats this page's progression must make, derived from its purpose. A page states its arc before it selects a single unit.
- **Beat assignment** — which beat each unit serves. One unit may serve one beat; a beat may take more than one unit; a beat with no unit is a gap in the arc, and a unit with no beat is a unit the page does not need.
- **Arc completeness** — that the declared arc is actually traversed, in the declared order, and arrives.

**The arc is declared, never inherited.** Different pages carry different arcs because they exist for different reasons, and two pages in different verticals should not be expected to share one. A page whose reader already knows the problem does not open on the problem. The arc belongs to the page's purpose, and it is read off that purpose rather than off a list of arcs that have worked before.

**This does not reopen the rule against convention (§6).** The two are easy to mistake for each other and are opposites. §6 forbids importing a familiar *arrangement of parts* because it is familiar. An arc imports no parts: it names the advances the progression owes the reader, and leaves entirely open which units make them, in what form, and how many. A declared arc constrains selection — it is the thing a unit must justify itself against — where a convention *supplies* selection and asks nothing. The failure §6 names is a sequence nobody chose; the failure this section names is a shape nobody declared. A page that adopts an arc because it is the usual arc has violated §6 through this section, not by means of it.

**The failure when ignored.** When no arc is declared, the page is assembled from units that are each defensible and collectively unaccountable. Nothing can be shown to be missing, because nothing was promised; nothing can be shown to be surplus, because there is no shape it fails to serve. The result is the page that reads as complete and lands as forgettable — every part justified, the whole unaccounted for.

## §6 — Encounter Sequencing

**The governing truth.** Information must be *encountered* in an order, and that order is decided — derived from the page's purpose, the communication requirements, the information's own dependencies, and the progression's objectives. Sequence is the act of turning a set of advances into a path actually walked.

**Why it exists.** Dependency makes some orders impossible and purpose makes some orders wrong, but neither hands over the order itself. A reader cannot grasp what builds on a foundation they have not yet been given; cannot be asked to decide before they understand. Sequencing is where the progression becomes a definite path — the layer's most consequential and most exposed act.

**The behaviors that emerge.** This layer determines:

- **Encounter Order** — which advance is met before which, across the whole progression.
- **Encounter Timing** — the *structural position* at which a piece of information enters the progression: early and foundational, or late and reinforcing. (This is position within the unfolding, **not** scroll speed, dwell, motion, or attention pacing — those behavioral mechanics belong to `PHILOSOPHY.md`, not here.)
- **Foundational Encounters** — the advances that must be met first, because everything after depends on them.
- **Supporting Encounters** — the advances that strengthen an understanding already made possible.
- **Reinforcing Encounters** — the advances that confirm or deepen what has already been established.

This is where the work Information Architecture deliberately stopped short of is carried out. Information Architecture emitted the dependency constraints — *derived information may not be encountered before its foundation* — but did not choose the order. This layer chooses it, **within** those constraints and **within** the narrative the layers above mandate (`PURPOSE.md` §4, `PHILOSOPHY.md` §10). To honor the constraint is inherited; to choose the sequence is this layer's own work.

**The governing rule against convention.** *Sequence emerges from dependencies, purpose, and progression objectives — it does not emerge from convention.* The order is derived, every time, from the page's purpose, its communication requirements, its information dependencies, and its progression objectives — never defaulted to a familiar arrangement because it is familiar. The moment a sequence is chosen because it is the customary shape rather than because the progression requires it, this layer has stopped being purpose-driven and become pattern-driven, and the progression no longer belongs to the page it serves.

**The failure when ignored.** When sequencing is left to convention or chance, the reader is asked to build on foundations not yet laid, or to decide before they understand; the advances arrive out of the order comprehension requires, and the progression cannot assemble the understanding its pieces would otherwise have supported.

## §7 — Progression Units

**The governing truth.** A progression is divided into **units** — distinct stages of advancement, each one a single step the reader takes toward understanding, belief, or decision confidence. A progression unit is a *conceptual stage within the unfolding*, defined entirely by the advance it makes.

**Why it exists.** Progression that is not divided into stages cannot be reasoned about — it cannot be ordered, connected, or checked for whether it advances. Units are what make the unfolding a structure rather than an undivided flow. But a unit must exist for the right reason, and the reason is the whole of this section: **A Progression Unit exists because progression requires a distinct advancement in understanding, belief, or decision confidence — not because content must be grouped.** Grouping related information is **Information Architecture's** work, already done; a progression unit is justified only when the progression itself requires a distinct step forward. If two bodies of related information make a single advance, they are one unit; if one body of information makes two advances, it is two.

**The behaviors that emerge.** This layer determines:

- What constitutes a meaningful unit of progression — the boundary of a single advance.
- Where one advance ends and the next begins — set by the progression, not by the information's affinities and not by any visual grouping.
- That every unit makes a genuine advance — a unit that moves the reader nowhere is not a unit.

A progression unit is **not** a layout, **not** a component, and **not** a visual section. It is a stage in the unfolding, named before any spatial decision exists, and a section is at most one way that stage might later be realized.

**The failure when ignored.** When units are drawn around grouped content instead of around advances, this layer quietly recreates Information Architecture's clustering, and the progression fills with stages that group information without moving the reader — present, tidy, and going nowhere.

## §8 — Transition Logic

**The governing truth.** Progression units must *connect.* Between each unit and the next there is a logic that carries the reader across the boundary — that makes the next advance follow from the last rather than merely come after it. Transition logic is the connective tissue of the progression, and without it a sequence of units is a list, not a path.

**Why it exists.** Two advances placed in order do not, by that alone, become a progression; the reader must be carried from one to the other. A unit that does not follow from what precedes it forces the reader to start again, and the progression fractures at the seam. Transition logic is what makes the unfolding continuous.

**The behaviors that emerge.** This layer determines, for each connection between units, the logic that carries it — among the recurring kinds:

- **Continuation** — the next unit extends the line of the last.
- **Escalation** — the next unit raises the stakes or deepens the commitment.
- **Validation** — the next unit substantiates what the last asserted.
- **Expansion** — the next unit broadens what the last introduced.
- **Resolution** — the next unit closes what the last opened.
- **Reinforcement** — the next unit confirms and strengthens what is already held.

This is connective *logic*, not visual transition and not motion. How a connection is later expressed — any easing, any movement — belongs to the layers below and to `PHILOSOPHY.md`; this layer decides only that the advances connect, and by what logic.

**The failure when ignored.** When transition logic is absent, units sit adjacent but disconnected; the reader is dropped between advances and made to reassemble the thread, and a progression that should have flowed instead stalls at every seam.

## §9 — Progression Objectives

**The governing truth.** Every progression unit exists to contribute something to the page's purpose — a definite objective it serves within the larger progression. A unit's objective is the reason it is in the progression at all, and the measure of whether it earns its place.

**Why it exists.** A progression is only as purposeful as its parts. A unit with no clear objective is a unit doing no identifiable work, and it dilutes the progression by occupying a place that advancement should have. Objectives are what keep every part of the unfolding accountable to the whole.

**The behaviors that emerge.** This layer determines:

- What each unit contributes to the page's purpose — the specific advance it is responsible for.
- How each unit's objective serves the progression as a whole — its role in reaching the destination.
- That no unit is without an objective — a unit that contributes nothing identifiable is unjustified and removed.

These objectives are governing principles, not fixed roles tied to any kind of page; the objective of a unit is whatever advance the specific progression requires of it, never a convention imported from how pages are usually built.

**The failure when ignored.** When units carry no objectives, the progression accumulates stages that occupy the path without advancing it; the reader is moved through more without being brought nearer, and the progression's purpose is diluted by everything in it that serves no purpose.

## §10 — Progression Integrity

**The governing truth.** A Progression Model must remain **coherent and complete** — it must actually move the reader, without gaps, contradictions, or stalls, from the beginning of the unfolding to its destination. Integrity is the property of the whole progression holding together as a single advancing path.

**Why it exists.** A progression assembled from sound units and sound transitions can still fail as a whole — it can double back, contradict itself, leave a gap the reader cannot cross, or move without advancing. Integrity is the discipline of checking the progression *as a system*, not just its parts, before any page is built on it.

**The behaviors that emerge.** This layer guards the progression against the ways it loses coherence:

- **Missing progression** — a required advance the model never makes.
- **Broken progression** — a point where the path does not actually carry the reader forward.
- **Redundant progression** — advancement repeated with nothing added.
- **Conflicting progression** — two parts of the unfolding that pull against each other.
- **Premature progression** — an advance asked for before the reader is prepared to make it.

**The governing concept — Progressional Sufficiency.** Information Architecture asks *is the Information Model sufficient to support understanding?* This layer asks the parallel question one level down: **does the Progression Model contain sufficient progression to support the required understanding, belief, and action?** Information completeness and progression completeness are different things. An Information Model can be wholly complete — every required piece present, every relationship mapped, every dependency sound — and the Progression Model built from it can still be *weak*: the information is all there, yet the unfolding fails to guide the reader to the understanding it could have supported. Progressional Sufficiency is the question only this layer can answer, and the cleanest statement of its distinct identity: sufficient progression, not merely sufficient information.

**The failure when ignored.** When integrity goes unchecked, a progression that looks finished ships with a gap, a contradiction, or a stall inside it, and the understanding it was built to assemble fails at exactly the point the progression stopped advancing.

## §11 — What Page Structure must never do

This section is the layer's discipline, and it is absolute. Page Structure explicitly rejects ownership of everything around it. For each, the decision belongs to a named layer, and this layer claims none of it.

**Not information discovery.** It does not create information, remove information, or redefine information. The Information Model is settled before this layer begins. Those belong to **Information Architecture** (`INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md`). This layer sequences information; it never authors it.

**Not information relationships.** It does not alter relationships or redefine dependencies. Those are facts about the information, owned by **Information Architecture**. This layer orders *within* the dependency constraints it inherits; it never rewrites them.

**Not communication strategy.** It does not decide meaning, communication modes, communication mediums, belief strategy, or experience strategy. Those belong to **Communication & Perception** (`COMMUNICATION_AND_PERCEPTION_SYSTEM.md`). This layer decides how information unfolds; it never decides what must happen in the mind.

**Not pacing mechanics.** It does not decide the behavioral mechanics of pacing, attention, or scroll — how a progression is physically carried down a rendered page. Those belong to **`PHILOSOPHY.md`**. This layer decides the *structural* order and connective logic of the unfolding; how that unfolding is paced and felt in motion is governed above.

**Not layout.** It does not decide columns, widths, alignment, density, distribution, or containment. Those belong to **Section Layout** (`SECTION_LAYOUT.md`).

**Not components.** It does not decide cards, tables, tabs, accordions, or carousels. Those belong to the **Component System** (`COMPONENT_SYSTEM.md`, executed by `component-governance/components.md`, `COMPOSITION.md`, `COMPOSE.md`).

**Not visual language.** It does not decide typography, colour, contrast, motion, or visual hierarchy. Those belong to **Visual Language** (`VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md`, executed by `rulebooks/visual-language.md`).

**Not brand expression.** It does not decide personality, character, or brand expression. Those belong to **Brand Expression** (`BRAND_EXPRESSION.md`, executed by `rulebooks/design-charter.md` and `creatives.md`) and, above all, to the Creative Director.

The rule beneath every line of this section is the one boundary from the header: this layer governs progression; it never creates the substance above it nor decides the form below it.

## §12 — The output of Page Structure

The output of this layer is a single artifact: the **Progression Model.** It comprises:

- **Progression Units** — the meaningful stages of advancement.
- **Encounter Sequence** — the order in which those stages are encountered.
- **Transition Logic** — the connective logic that carries the reader between stages.
- **Progression Objectives** — what each stage contributes to the page's purpose.

It never outputs layouts, sections, components, or visual systems. The output remains *progressional* from end to end. **Section Layout is the layer that converts this Progression Model into spatial form** — it is the consumer of this layer's artifact, not part of it. When this layer hands down a coherent, sufficient Progression Model, everything below it has a path it can give form to and did not have to find.

## §13 — Failure modes

Each failure below is a violation of a principle established above; the cure is the principle.

- **Missing Progression** — a required advance is never made; understanding has no path to it (violates §5, §10).
- **Broken Progression** — a point in the unfolding does not actually carry the reader forward; the path stops without arriving (violates §5, §8).
- **Circular Progression** — the unfolding doubles back on itself, returning the reader to where they were rather than advancing them (violates §5, §10).
- **Premature Understanding** — an advance is asked for before the reader has been prepared to make it; the progression builds on ground it has not yet laid (violates §6, §8).
- **Unsupported Progression** — an advance is attempted without the prior advances it depends on; the dependency constraints inherited from Information Architecture were not honored in the order (violates §6).
- **Redundant Progression** — advancement is repeated with nothing added; the reader is moved through the same step twice (violates §9, §10).
- **Progression Gaps** — a step is missing between two advances; the reader is asked to cross a distance the progression never bridges (violates §8, §10).
- **Progression Conflicts** — two parts of the unfolding pull against each other, so advancing in one undoes the other (violates §9, §10).
- **Undeclared Arc** — the progression has no declared shape, so its units can only be judged one at a time. Every part is defensible, nothing can be shown to be missing because nothing was promised, and nothing can be shown to be surplus because there is no shape it fails to serve (violates §5.1).
- **Unserved Beat** — the arc declares an advance the page never makes; the progression promises a shape and delivers a gap in it (violates §5.1, §10).
- **Unbeated Unit** — a unit carries a genuine objective and serves no beat of the declared arc. It survives the objectives test (§9) and still does not belong: contributing something is not the same as being needed (violates §5.1).
- **Stagnant Progression** — the most distinctly Page-Structure failure: information continues to be encountered but understanding, belief, or decision confidence does not meaningfully advance. It is *not* redundancy — redundancy repeats information; stagnant progression may introduce wholly *new* information and still fail to move the reader forward. The page keeps presenting and the reader keeps reading, yet no advance is made — the information is present and non-repeating, and the progression has nonetheless stalled (violates §5, §10, and is the precise failure Progressional Sufficiency exists to prevent).

## §14 — Non-Negotiables

These are immutable. They are permanent, universal, technology-agnostic, industry-agnostic, and medium-agnostic. They define what Page Structure fundamentally is and is not, and no downstream need or upstream pressure may override them.

1. **Page Structure determines how information is progressively encountered — never what the information is, and never how it appears.** The unfolding is its whole territory.
2. **Its output is a Progression Model — never a page, layout, section, component, or visual.** The output is progressional from end to end.
3. **Progression exists before the page.** A page is one realization of a progression; this layer governs progression, not page composition.
4. **It creates neither meaning nor information.** It transforms a settled Information Model into purposeful advancement; substance is settled above.
5. **Sequence emerges from dependencies, purpose, and progression objectives — never from convention.** The layer is purpose-driven, not pattern-driven.
5a. **A progression declares its arc before the page selects anything, and the arc is read off the page's purpose — never off a list of arcs that have worked before.** Every unit serves a named beat; a unit that serves none does not belong. This constrains selection and never supplies it, which is what keeps it on the right side of NN5.
6. **A progression unit exists because progression requires a distinct advancement — never because content must be grouped.** Grouping is Information Architecture's; advancement is this layer's.
7. **The purpose of progression is purposeful advancement, not movement.** A progression that moves without advancing has failed.
8. **The Progression Model must be sufficient, not merely built on sufficient information.** Complete information can still yield a weak progression; progressional sufficiency is checked on its own terms.
9. **It executes the laws above it, never contradicts them.** It operationalizes Purpose §4, Philosophy §10, and the constraints of Information Architecture §8; it does not re-author or overrule them.

## §15 — The final governing principle

Communication & Perception determines what must happen in the mind. Information Architecture determines what information must exist. Page Structure determines how that information unfolds.

It creates neither meaning nor information. It creates **progression** — it transforms an Information Model into a Progression Model that enables the required understanding, belief, and action while remaining faithful to the page's purpose and intent.

If any section of this document begins to discuss layouts, components, visuals, styling, interaction patterns, or implementation details, it has left its territory and must return to progression, encounter order, transition logic, progression objectives, and progression integrity. That return is always available, because those are the whole of what this layer governs, and the whole of what it must.
