Meridian is a month-end close automation platform for mid-market finance teams. The page's one job is to
convince a skeptical, skimming finance leader that the manual close is quietly costing them days every
month, and that Meridian removes that cost by automating reconciliation on top of the tools they already
run — no rip-and-replace.

Open on the felt pain of the manual close. Move into how Meridian plugs into the existing ledger and bank
feeds and reconciles automatically. Prove it with specific, checkable outcomes. Close on a single,
low-friction call to action framed as the reader's gain.

Avoid hype, superlatives, and jargon. The argument is concrete outcomes, shown before they are claimed.
