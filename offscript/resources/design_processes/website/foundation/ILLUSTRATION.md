# ILLUSTRATION — The Design Discipline of the Creative in a Composition

> **What this is.** The Foundation discipline that owns **how a creative occupies a composition** — how
> much authority it holds, how it meets what surrounds it, and whether it is the thing being shown or
> the environment something is shown in. It governs the creative's role in the design, never the
> creative itself. Its output is not an image, a treatment or a file; it is **compositional intent for
> the creative**.
>
> **What this is not.** Not the making of creative work. This system does not produce photography,
> illustration, artwork or product interfaces — those are made by people, and that boundary is
> absolute and is not this discipline's to reconsider. Not layout: how much room a section has is
> settled in `SECTION_LAYOUT.md`. Not surface material, which governs a field's substance. Not the
> small reduced mark, which is Iconography. It names no asset and no treatment value.
>
> **The one boundary above all others.** *Illustration determines how a creative occupies a
> composition — its authority, its edge, and its role. It never makes a creative, never describes one,
> never modifies one, and never decides what a section says. It governs compositional intent for the
> creative, and nothing else.*
>
> **The test every line passes.** *Would this still be true if every asset in the library were
> replaced, while creatives still occupied compositions the same way?*
>
> **Where this sits.** A Design Discipline of the Website Foundation, reasoned through after
> `EDITORIAL_ART_DIRECTION.md` — which tells it what the creative must contribute emotionally. **On any
> conflict, Governance wins, and this document wins over `rulebooks/creatives.md`, which
> operationalizes it.**

## §0 — First principles

A composition containing a creative is not a composition with an image added to it. It is a different
composition, because a creative is the strongest single element most compositions will ever hold: it
is found before type, resolved before text, and remembered after both.

Systems nonetheless treat creatives as insertions. A layout is designed, a space remains, an image is
placed in it. Every step is reasonable and the sequence produces one predictable outcome — a creative
sized to whatever room was left over, holding whatever authority that leftover happened to confer. The
strongest element in the composition was assigned its weight by accident.

The inversion this discipline insists on is therefore not a stylistic preference but a correction of
ordering: **the creative's role is settled before the surrounding layout, and the layout adapts to
it.** A creative fitted to available space has had its authority decided by a decision that was not
about it.

There is a second, quieter failure that only appears once the first is fixed. A system can declare a
creative's authority correctly and still render it powerless — because authority declared as a share
of *something* is only meaningful if the something is the composition. A creative declared dominant,
placed inside a narrow column, and sized as a fraction of that column, is dominant of nothing. The
declaration was made, honoured arithmetically, and produced the exact outcome it was written to
prevent.

This is the discipline's most important lesson about itself: an intent that is realized against the
wrong reference is worse than an intent never stated, because it reports success.

## §1 — Identity

Illustration is the discipline of **the creative's role in a composition**.

It owns three questions, and no other discipline asks any of them: how much of the composition the
creative commands, how it meets what surrounds it, and whether it is a subject or an environment.

It is deliberately not the discipline of *making* creative work. That production boundary is settled
above and is not reopened here. What remains — and what nothing else owns — is everything about how a
creative that already exists takes its place in a design.

## §2 — Mission

Illustration exists so that **every creative holds exactly the authority it was given, visibly, and
the composition was built around it rather than around the space it was fitted into.**

Success is a composition where a creative's importance is legible without reading anything, matches
what was declared, and could not have been produced by placing an image in a gap.

## §3 — Constitutional Responsibility

This discipline owns **compositional intent for the creative**: its authority, its edge, and its role
as subject or environment.

**What it inherits.** What the creative must contribute emotionally, from Editorial Art Direction. The
spatial allocation, from Section Layout. The communication objective, from Communication & Perception.
The available assets, from the approved library — which this discipline selects from and never adds
to.

**What it contributes.** The authority a creative holds, expressed against the composition; the
vocabulary of edges by which a creative meets the page; and the subject-or-environment determination
that decides how it is treated.

**What it rejects.** It does not make a creative. It does not describe one — a prose brief for a
photograph is creative direction, which this system has withdrawn. It does not modify one: every
treatment is applied by the system above an untouched asset. It does not decide what a section
communicates. It does not decide how much room the section has.

## §4 — Core Question

> **What authority does this creative hold in this composition, and is that authority visible?**

The second clause exists because the first has been answered correctly before and produced nothing.
Declared authority that is not perceptible is not authority; it is a record of an intention.

## §5 — Inputs

- **The felt register** (Editorial Art Direction) — what the creative must contribute emotionally.
- **The Spatial Model** — the room the section holds, and its compositional character.
- **The Communication Model** — what the section must make understood, which decides whether the
  creative is evidence, subject or atmosphere.
- **The approved library** — what exists. Selection only; never addition.

## §6 — Transformation

> **A creative and a communication objective → the creative's role in the composition.**

What enters is an available creative and a section that needs something from it. What is applied is
the judgment of how much authority it should hold, how it should meet the page, and what job it is
doing. What emerges is compositional intent — settled *before* the surrounding layout, which then
adapts to it.

## §7 — Outputs

- **Authority** — how much of the composition the creative commands.
- **Framing** — the edge by which it meets what surrounds it.
- **Subject determination** — whether it is the thing shown or the environment.
- **Reasoning** — why this creative, at this authority, in this composition.

It outputs no asset, no crop and no treatment value.

## §8 — Discipline Knowledge

### The creative decides the layout, never the reverse

The ordering claim, and the one everything else depends on. A creative's proportion, authority and
placement are settled first; the surrounding composition is then built to hold them. Reversing this
produces a creative sized to leftover space, which is how the strongest element in a composition ends
up with an authority nobody chose.

The test is procedural rather than visual: if the creative's role could not have been stated before
the layout existed, the layout decided it.

### Authority must be expressed against the composition, not against a container

The subtlest and most consequential idea here. A creative's authority is a claim about the *whole
composition* — it is the statement that this element commands the field. If that claim is realized as
a share of whatever box the creative happens to sit inside, the claim quietly becomes relative to an
arbitrary reference.

A creative declared dominant, sitting inside a narrow column, sized as a share of that column,
occupies a small fraction of the composition it was declared dominant of. Nothing failed. The
declaration was made and honoured arithmetically, and the result is the precise outcome the
declaration existed to prevent.

This generalizes past this discipline: **an intent realized against the wrong reference is worse than
no intent, because it reports success.**

### Authority is more than width

Authority is how much a creative commands, and width is only one contributor. A creative surrounded by
generous emptiness commands more than a wider one crowded by text. A creative that breaks an edge
commands more than one held inside a margin. A creative that types sit *around* commands more than one
type sits beside.

A discipline that governs authority as width alone will produce creatives that are technically large
and compositionally weak — which is a recognizable outcome and a common one.

### A creative must meet the page somewhere, and that meeting is a decision

Every creative has an edge, and something happens at it. It can be held within a boundary, run past
one, be framed by a mark, sit within a window onto something larger, or be overlapped by another
element.

Left ungoverned, that edge defaults to the simplest available treatment — a plain rectangle — and a
page of plain rectangles reads as a page of images placed in slots, regardless of how carefully each
was chosen. **The absence of a framing decision is itself a framing decision**, and it is the one that
reads as least considered.

Framing is not decoration on a creative. It is the statement of how the creative relates to the page:
whether it belongs to the composition, sits within it, or breaks out of it.

### Subject and environment are different jobs for the same asset

One asset can be the thing being shown or the field something else is shown against, and the two
demand opposite treatment. A subject is presented at full strength — dimming it would dim the thing
being shown. An environment sits behind content and must yield to it, which frequently means measured
mitigation so the content remains readable.

The determination is not a property of the asset. It is a property of the *job*, and the same file can
take either. A system that treats it as a property of the file will either veil subjects that should
be shown or leave environments competing with the text on them.

### An environment carrying text invalidates the contrast that was measured without it

Text contrast is established against a known field. Placing a photograph behind that text destroys the
measurement, and the failure is silent: nothing in the declared styles changed, and the text may be
unreadable.

An environment used as a surface therefore carries whatever mitigation restores the measurement it
replaced — and that mitigation follows the composition, holding where content sits and releasing where
the environment breathes, rather than flatly dimming the asset.

### A reserved position is a claim about the future, not about the present

Where the required creative does not exist, the position is reserved rather than faked. A reservation
is honest — it declares an absence instead of concealing one.

But a reservation inherits everything a real creative would have held: the same authority, the same
edge, the same role. A reservation sized to the room available has stopped reserving a creative's
position and started decorating a gap — and it will read as an orphan, because it is one. **The
reservation represents the future creative. It never represents the available space.**

## §9 — Organizational Design System Decisions

- **This system selects; it never generates.** Where a creative exists in the approved library it is
  selected; where it does not, the position is reserved. Adopted because production is outside this
  system's boundary and selection is a layout decision it legitimately makes.
- **Assets are never modified.** Every treatment is applied above an untouched file. Adopted so one
  asset can read two ways in two compositions, and so a per-page effect is never mistaken for the
  material.
- **Proportion comes from a closed set.** Adopted because a repeated small vocabulary of proportions
  is itself part of the visual identity, recognized through recurrence.
- **A real asset is cropped to an approved proportion at a governed focus that travels with the
  asset.** Adopted because a centre crop is the crop nobody chose, and because preserving a proportion
  is not a licence to ship an off-vocabulary one.
- **Authority, edge and role are settled before the surrounding layout.** Adopted as the ordering the
  discipline exists to enforce.
- **A reservation inherits the full role of the creative it stands for.** Adopted because a
  reservation sized to available space reintroduces the failure the whole discipline prevents.

## §10 — Primary Primitives

**Presence — how much of the composition the creative commands.** Deliberately named *presence* rather
than scale, because what is governed is compositional authority rather than size, and deliberately not
*dominance*, which is load-bearing upstream and frequently not what a creative holds. Presence is a
share **of the composition** — the reference is part of the primitive, and getting it wrong is how the
primitive silently stops working.

**Framing — how the creative meets what surrounds it.** The edge condition: held, breaking, marked,
windowed, overlapped. Framing is the primitive that determines whether a creative reads as belonging
to the composition or as placed into it, and the one most often left unset — at which point it
defaults to the treatment that reads as least considered.

**Subject — whether the creative is the thing shown or the environment something is shown in.** The
primitive that decides treatment, mitigation and legibility obligations. It is a property of the job
rather than of the asset, and the same file takes either.

The three are independent and jointly sufficient: presence without framing is an image at the right
size with no relationship to the page; framing without presence is a well-edged element of unknown
importance; and neither means anything until subject determines what the creative is *for*.

## §11 — Decision Intelligence

**Settle the creative's role before the layout exists.** If the role could not have been stated first,
the layout decided it and the ordering has already been lost.

**Check what the authority is expressed against.** A share is meaningless without its reference, and
the reference must be the composition. This is the discipline's most likely silent failure and worth
verifying explicitly rather than assuming.

**Reason about authority as command, not as width.** Surrounding emptiness, edge treatment and the
relationship to type all contribute. A creative can be widened and made weaker.

**Choose the edge deliberately, including when the answer is a plain one.** A plain edge chosen is
different from a plain edge defaulted to, and only one of them survives being asked why.

**Ask what job the creative is doing before deciding how to treat it.** Subject and environment are
opposite treatments, and the asset does not answer the question.

**Where the creative does not exist, reserve at full role.** A reservation that shrinks to fit
available space is the failure this discipline prevents, arriving through the one path that looks
responsible.

**Let the register decide what the creative must contribute.** Editorial Art Direction has settled
what the moment should feel like. A creative that is atmospherically wrong for its moment is wrong
regardless of how well it is placed.

**Change the layout before compromising the creative.** If an approved proportion will not fit, the
layout is what yields. Inventing a proportion to fit a space is the ordering failure in its most
concrete form.

## §12 — Behaviour

The discipline behaves like an art director laying out a page rather than a developer filling a slot:
it decides what the picture is doing before deciding where it goes, it is willing to rebuild a layout
around one element, and it treats a weak placement of a strong asset as a failure of the composition
rather than of the asset. It is decisive about authority and unromantic about assets — a creative that
is not doing a job does not appear.

## §13 — Cross-Discipline Relationships

- **Editorial Art Direction, consulted before.** Says what the creative must contribute emotionally.
  This discipline decides authority, edge and role; it never re-decides register.
- **Section Layout, above.** Decides how much room the section holds. This discipline decides how the
  creative occupies it — and where the two conflict, the layout adapts, because that is the ordering
  the Spatial Model itself mandates.
- **Surface.** Where a creative is used as an *environment*, Surface governs its material and
  recession. The seam is the creative's job, which this discipline determines.
- **Colour.** A creative carries its own colour, which is never modified. Where a creative sits behind
  text, the contrast floor still applies and this discipline owes the mitigation that restores it.
- **Iconography.** The seam is responsibility, not size: a mark *points* at meaning that exists, a
  creative *carries* meaning.
- **Spacing.** Owns the intervals around a creative — which contribute to its authority, so the two
  disciplines must agree. This one states the authority required; Spacing realizes the emptiness.
- **`rulebooks/creatives.md`, below.** Operationalizes this expertise into the closed vocabularies of
  proportion, presence, placement and framing, and the reservation doctrine. It inherits this
  reasoning and never authors it.

## §14 — Quality Intelligence

- Could this creative's role have been stated before the layout existed?
- What is its authority expressed against — the composition, or the box it happens to sit in?
- Is the declared authority *visible*? Would a reader agree about which element commands this
  composition?
- Was the edge chosen, or did it default?
- Is this creative a subject or an environment, and is it treated as the one it is?
- If this is a reservation, does it hold the full role of the creative it stands for?
- Did the layout adapt to this creative, or did this creative fit the layout?

## §15 — Failure Modes

**The creative fitted to leftover space.** The founding failure. The strongest element in the
composition assigned its authority by a decision that was not about it.

**Authority against the wrong reference.** A share computed against a container rather than the
composition. Declared correctly, honoured arithmetically, and producing exactly what the declaration
was written to prevent — while reporting success.

**Authority as width alone.** Presence governed as size, ignoring emptiness, edge and relationship to
type. Produces creatives that are large and weak.

**The defaulted edge.** Framing never decided, so every creative is a plain rectangle. Reads as images
placed in slots however well each was chosen.

**Subject veiled, environment unmitigated.** The job misread. Either the thing being shown is dimmed,
or text sits unreadably on a photograph while every declared style checks out.

**The shrunken reservation.** A reserved position sized to available space. Reads as an orphan,
because it is one.

**Invention at the boundary.** A creative described, generated or substituted because the real one is
unavailable. The production boundary crossed through the path that looks most responsible.

**Proportion invented to fit.** An off-vocabulary ratio because the space demanded it. The layout
dictating the creative, in its most concrete form — and the near-miss is hardest to see.

## §16 — Verification

**Ordering.** The creative's role is declared, and the declaration is machine-readable rather than
inferred from the render.

**Reference.** Measured occupied area matches declared authority **against the composition** — the
check that catches the silent failure, and the one that must exist for the primitive to mean anything.

**Framing.** Every creative carries a framing treatment drawn from the closed set. A defaulted edge
must be able to fail.

**Vocabulary.** Proportion, presence and placement are on-vocabulary, and an inline value fails even
where its magnitude is approved.

**Role.** Subject and environment are distinguished, and mitigation follows the role.

**Selection.** Every asset comes from the approved library; no handle accepts an arbitrary source.

**Reservation integrity.** A reservation holds the same role as the creative it stands for.

## §17 — Evolution

The discipline matures by understanding composition better, not by acquiring more assets or more
treatments. A larger framing vocabulary is a weaker one: framing reads as a language through
recurrence, and a set large enough to always have a perfect fit is a set no reader recognizes.

It grows by getting more precise about what makes authority perceptible, by learning which framings
readers actually distinguish, and by finding that two treatments were always one. It does not grow by
following image-treatment trends, which change what an edge looks like and nothing about why a
creative commands a composition.

**A note on the focal-weight violations, because the assignment is not clean.** "Carried by its
creative, but the contract makes typography focal" is a *relational* failure between this discipline
and Typography: either the creative commands too much or the type commands too little, and the
measurement alone cannot say which. It is claimed here because this discipline owns the actionable
half — the creative's authority is what this system can currently reason about — and because
**Typography is not yet authored**. When it is, this row is shared, and the seam between them should
be stated in both. Recording it as jointly owned rather than silently assigning it to one discipline
is the honest form; leaving it unclaimed, as it was until this audit, is not.

section requiring a creative it does not have is the **archetype contract**'s; the intervals producing
a creative's surrounding emptiness are **Spacing**'s to realize.
