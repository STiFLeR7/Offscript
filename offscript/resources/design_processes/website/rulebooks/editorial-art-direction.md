# Editorial Art Direction — the register vocabulary (Rulebook · HOW)

> **Parent discipline: `foundation/EDITORIAL_ART_DIRECTION.md`.** This rulebook operationalizes that
> expertise for production. It inherits the reasoning and never authors it: it may name, organize and
> sequence the vocabulary, but the moment it changes *why* a register exists it has overstepped
> (`FOUNDATION.md` §14). Every "why" below is a reference back, not a new argument.
>
> **Brand-agnostic.** Governs *how felt intent is declared and checked*. No value, colour, type size or
> component appears here — those belong to the disciplines that realize a register.
>
> **What this adds to production.** A closed vocabulary of registers, the band each Section Composition
> Archetype permits, and the contract a declaration must satisfy. Before this, felt quality was
> inferred separately by every discipline; after it, they read one declaration.

---

## The rule, in one line

> **Every section declares one register, from the closed set, from within the band its archetype
> permits — and no two adjacent sections declare the same one.**

---

## §1 — The register vocabulary (closed — eight, and only these eight)

A register is a composite of the three Primary Primitives — **Intensity** (how much force the moment
carries), **Temperature** (how human it feels), **Restraint** (how much is withheld). The primitive
columns are **rationale**: they explain why registers differ and are not separately declared or
checked. The register name is the **predicate**.

| Register | Intensity | Temperature | Restraint | The moment it names |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **editorial** | moderate | neutral | high | composed and considered; the page speaking in its own voice |
| **quiet** | lowest | neutral | highest | deliberate stillness; a moment given room rather than filled |
| **immersive** | high | warm | high | the reader inside the moment rather than observing it |
| **technical** | raised | cool | low | precision on display; denser, more exact, more to inspect |
| **human** | low | warm | moderate | being spoken to rather than shown; a person on the other side |
| **confident** | raised | neutral–warm | high | a claim made plainly and left standing without hedging |
| **narrative** | rising | neutral | moderate | a sequence accumulating; no part is the destination |
| **instructional** | moderate | cool | moderate | being shown how; sequence in service of doing |
| **reflective** | low | warm | high | a pause to consider what was just established |
| **closing** | highest | warm | high | one thing asked, once; the page is over |

**The set is closed.** An eleventh register is not authored because a section did not obviously fit —
that is how a vocabulary inflates until it governs nothing (parent §17). A section that fits none of
the ten is a finding to report, not a licence to extend.

**The vocabulary was replaced on 2026-07-29; the mechanism was not** (`DECISION-REGISTER.md` DR-12).
The creative direction named ten section personalities, and they are the register set now. Everything
around them is unchanged: the permitted-band table, the adjacency predicate, and the
archetype-constrains / instance-decides shape resolved in DR-6. A creative director naming the felt
registers of their own work is the intended way this vocabulary changes — what would not be
legitimate is a register appearing because one section was awkward to classify.

The prior set (`arrival` · `disquiet` · `evidence` · `assurance` · `momentum` · `immersion` ·
`address` · `repose`) maps onto the new one without loss: `immersion`→`immersive`,
`assurance`→`human`, `momentum`→`narrative`, `evidence`→`technical`, `address`→`closing`,
`repose`→`quiet`. Two do not survive as names and are worth stating rather than quietly dropping —
**`arrival`** is now carried by `editorial` or `immersive` depending on how the page opens, and
**`disquiet`** by `reflective` or `confident` depending on whether the unresolved problem is being
sat with or stated. The new set is less about the reader's position in the page and more about the
page's own voice, which is what a creative direction is entitled to decide.

## §2 — Permitted bands (which registers each archetype may take)

The archetype constrains; the instance decides. This is the shape the system already uses for creative
presence, where `media-first` permits `l`–`xl` and each section takes one value from the band.

| Archetype | Permitted registers |
|---|---|
| **immersive-landscape** | `immersive` · `editorial` |
| **editorial-split** | `editorial` · `confident` · `human` |
| **editorial-stack** | `editorial` · `quiet` · `reflective` · `confident` · `closing` |
| **text-first** | `confident` · `human` |
| **media-first** | `immersive` · `editorial` |
| **product-showcase** | `technical` · `confident` |
| **storytelling-composition** | `narrative` · `human` · `immersive` |
| **narrative-flow** | `narrative` · `instructional` |
| **proof-layout** | `technical` · `confident` |
| **feature-comparison** | `technical` · `instructional` |

**Every band holds at least two.** This is a hard constraint on the table, not an accident of it: a
band of one would make the adjacency rule in §3 unfailable for any two adjacent sections sharing that
archetype — a check that cannot fail, which is the defect this system spent a full phase finding.
`editorial-stack` carries the widest band because it is the general-purpose archetype and most often
repeats within one page.

**A band is a permission, not a ranking.** No register within a band is the default. Choosing by
position in this table rather than by what the progression needs is the local-optimization failure the
parent names in §15.

## §3 — The declaration contract

Four requirements. The first three are **predicates** — a render either satisfies them or fails. The
fourth is **rationale** and is explicitly marked so, because it is judgment and no measurement decides
it.

**P1 · A section declares exactly one register.** A section declaring none had its felt quality
decided by whatever its realization happened to produce, which is the condition this discipline exists
to end.

**P2 · The declared register is within the band its archetype permits.** A declaration outside the
band is a section claiming to communicate one way and to feel like another.

**P3 · No two adjacent sections declare the same register.** Contrast is what makes a register legible
at all (parent §8); repetition is this system's observed failure rather than a hypothetical one.
Adjacency is by document order, and it is the *sequence* that is checked, never the set — a page may
use `technical` four times, and may not use it twice in a row.

**R4 · The register is the one the progression needs here — rationale, not predicate.** Whether a
register is *correct*, as opposed to well-formed, is judgment. It is reported for human review and
never auto-failed. Marking this explicitly rather than shipping a check that passes on everything is
required by `COMPOSE.md` §A2.1: a field that is neither predicate nor marked rationale is a
declaration that constrains nothing.

## §4 — What this rulebook does not do

- **It does not realize a register.** Type, colour, spacing, surface, iconography and creative
  treatment each belong to their own discipline. A register tells them what the moment must feel like
  and nothing about how to achieve it.
- **It does not decide how much space a moment occupies.** Allocation, containment and density are
  settled in `SECTION_LAYOUT.md`. Density and intensity correlate and are not the same thing.
- **It does not re-author the Experience Character.** Identity constrains which registers are
  available; it never selects among them (parent §11).
- **It does not rank registers.** There is no strongest or best register, and `closing` is not a
  reward for a page that has earned it.
