How Meridian Cleared a 3-Week Backlog in Nine Days.

MH Meridian Health Healthcare
The situation

Prior-authorization requests piled up faster than a four-person team could read them. Turnaround stretched past three weeks, and clinicians felt it.

What changed

Acme read each request against policy, drafted the decision, and routed only the genuine edge cases to a human reviewer — inside the systems the team already used.

The result

Backlog cleared in nine days. The same team now handles double the volume with no added headcount.
