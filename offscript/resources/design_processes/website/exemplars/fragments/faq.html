How does this work with our existing systems?

Agents operate on top of the tools you already use — your ERP, CRM, ticketing, email, and spreadsheets — through APIs or the same screens your team uses. No rip-and-replace.

Is it secure and compliant?

Agents run inside your environment or a dedicated, access-scoped tenant. Every action is logged and auditable, and we support SOC 2, role-based access, and data-residency requirements.

How long until something is live?

The first agent is typically in production in about three weeks. We start with one high-value process, prove it, then expand from there.

What happens when an agent isn't sure?

It escalates. Agents act autonomously within the guardrails you set and hand off to a human the moment confidence drops — so the judgment calls stay with your team.

How is it priced?

Outcome-linked. We align our fee to the cost, time, or throughput we actually move — so you're buying results, not seats.

Will it disrupt my team's day-to-day?

No. Agents take the repetitive load while your people keep the exceptions and the relationships. Rollout runs alongside live operations.
