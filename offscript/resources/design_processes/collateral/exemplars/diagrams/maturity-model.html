The form is fixed by the family: a linear progression, a maximum of 5 levels, running Level 1 → Level 5. Ascent is carried by a rising baseline — the row is bottom-aligned and each stage takes a taller min-height, so the tops step up; tones deepen one .tone-* step per stage as a secondary cue. Directional connectors mark the progression — relationship only, no ornament. Flat fills, no gradients. Pure HTML/CSS — Flexbox, no JavaScript, no SVG, no absolute positioning.
