Quadrant · positioning on two axes (impact vs effort, risk vs value) (structured visualization, not a diagram). Governing rules: the Diagrams Rulebook (README-DIAGRAMS.md — §9 quadrant, §12 style, §13 integrity). Built as a grid-based 2×2: four equal cells, hairline-divided, with item nodes placed inside the correct cell by grid placement — never pixel coordinates. Axis labels live outside the 2×2 (a Y rail on the left, an X row beneath) so a plotted node can never overlap a label — the hard rule is labels always stay visible. One quadrant may take a subtle .tone-* wash to mark the priority zone; no gradients. Pure HTML/CSS — Grid, no JavaScript, no SVG, no absolute positioning. Doc-scoped (never in colors_and_type.css).
