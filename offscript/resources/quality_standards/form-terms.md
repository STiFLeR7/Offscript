# Form Terms — authority-class form vocabulary (W3-S2 Intent Readiness Floor)

> Cross-track governance for the Intent Readiness Floor (W3-S2). A *number bound to one
> of the length units below* is an authority-class **form overstep** — its presence in an
> Intent Brief marks the brief **not-ready** (the intent named form, which it must never do).
>
> Deliberately EXCLUDED because they false-positive on legitimate intent prose (see
> `docs/internals/W3-S2-CONTRACT-RECONCILIATION.md` U-FORM-NARROW):
> percentages (`20%` = a real success metric), bare counts (`the one action`),
> ambiguous-with-English units (`in`, `pc`, `s`, `deg`), named colors (`blue`), visual
> adjectives (`bold`, `centered`), and component/layout nouns (`hero`, `grid`, `column`).
> Those, where surfaced at all, are observability (S5), never the gate.
>
> Color literals (`#`-hex, `rgb()/hsl()`) are CSS-spec *patterns*, not a tunable lexicon —
> they live in code (`src/generate/intent-readiness.ts`), not here.

## Length units

- px
- pt
- rem
- em
- vh
- vw
- vmin
- vmax
- mm
- cm
