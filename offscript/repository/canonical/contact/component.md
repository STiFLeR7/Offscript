# contact

Full standalone contact-page body.

## Purpose

The full standalone contact page: the complete body of a dedicated contact page, gathering the ways to
reach the organization and the form to do so into one considered whole. It exists when contact is
important enough to warrant its own page — giving the conversation room, and assembling capture, methods,
and reassurance as the page's entire purpose rather than one band among many.

## Choose when

- Contact deserves its own dedicated page, not just a band within another.
- The organization has enough to say about reaching them to fill a considered page.
- A reader who navigates to "contact" expects a complete, self-sufficient destination.
- Assembling form, methods, and reassurance as a whole page serves the reader best.

## Avoid when

- A single band within another page is all that is needed — reach for the plain capture split.
- The capture should overlay an action elsewhere — reach for the overlay variant.
- The task is specifically a sales-led demo booking — reach for the booking band.
- The next step is a single freely-chosen action, not information — reach for a call-to-action role.

## Character

Low-friction and complete: the family's most expansive shape — a whole page given to opening a
conversation, assembling capture and contact methods and reassurance into a self-sufficient destination.
It keeps the family's restraint on the form itself while having room to be thorough about the ways to
reach out, and stays a welcoming door rather than a wall of fields.

## Composition

It is the entire body of a dedicated contact page, composing a capture form, the real ways to reach the
organization, and the reassurance around them into one coherent whole. The form must still ask only for
what it needs despite the extra room; the methods must be real; and the page must read as a complete
destination, not a padded single band.

## Contract

Assumes contact merits a dedicated page and that every method and field on it is real and honest. It
expects the extra space to be used for genuine completeness — real channels, honest expectations of
response — not for a bloated form; a whole page that over-asks, or lists channels that go unanswered,
wastes the destination a reader deliberately sought.

## Judgement

Used well, a reader who wanted to get in touch arrives at a complete, welcoming page and finds every way
to do so in one place. The tempting misuse is filling the page's room with unnecessary fields, or listing
contact routes that lead nowhere. Its value is a thorough, honest destination — use the space for real
completeness, not for friction.
