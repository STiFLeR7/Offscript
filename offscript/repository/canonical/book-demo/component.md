# book-demo

Sales-led demo-booking band.

## Purpose

The sales-led booking band: a demo-booking capture, for when the next step is to schedule time with the
team. It converts readiness into a booked conversation — gathering just enough to arrange the demo — for
offerings where a guided, sales-led walkthrough is how a serious reader is best moved toward a decision.

## Choose when

- The natural next step is booking a demo or a call with the team, not a general enquiry.
- The offering is best shown through a guided, sales-led walkthrough.
- A reader is serious enough to give time, and the ask is to schedule it.
- Capturing just enough to arrange the demo lowers the friction of saying yes.

## Avoid when

- The reader only wants general contact — reach for the plain capture split.
- The booking should appear as an overlay on another action — reach for the overlay variant.
- Both contact and newsletter are wanted — reach for the contact-plus-newsletter combo.
- The next step is a single freely-chosen action, not scheduling — reach for a call-to-action role.

## Character

Low-friction and sales-forward: a booking capture that turns interest into a scheduled demo, presented
so the cost of committing time feels small. It keeps the family's reassuring restraint — only what is
needed to book — while being unambiguously about arranging a conversation with the team, and honest
about what the demo will involve.

## Composition

It sits where a guided demo is the right next step, as a booking band that captures just enough to
schedule it. It must ask only what arranging the demo requires, place reassurance about the meeting
beside it, and be honest about what happens next — who will reach out, and what the demo covers — never
overstating to secure the booking.

## Contract

Assumes a real, staffed demo process and a reader ready to give time. It expects the ask to stay minimal
and the promise honest — a booking that leads to a genuine, prepared walkthrough, not a pretext for a
sales pitch the reader did not agree to; over-asking, or misrepresenting what the demo is, breaks the
trust the booking depends on.

## Judgement

Used well, a serious reader books time with the team at low friction and arrives expecting exactly what
they will get. The tempting misuse is demanding heavy qualification before the demo is earned, or
promising a walkthrough that turns into a hard sell. Its value is an honest, low-cost path to a real
conversation — keep the ask light and the promise true.
