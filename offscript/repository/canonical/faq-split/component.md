# faq-split

Long lists that must stay scannable.

## Purpose

The split-column FAQ: a long, expandable question-and-answer list arranged so it stays scannable even
when there are many entries. It is the family's heaviest shape, for the late moment when a reader
carries several real remaining doubts and each must be findable — the two-part layout keeping a long
list navigable rather than a wall to read through.

## Choose when

- Known objections stand between understanding and action, and there are enough of them to need real
  structure.
- The list is long and the reader must find their own question without reading all the others.
- The page can give the doubt-clearing beat substantial room before the close.
- The questions are genuine remaining doubts, answered honestly — not features in disguise.

## Avoid when

- The list is short and quiet — reach for a centered or single-column FAQ near the page end.
- Only a pointer is needed, with the real answers elsewhere — reach for the chip teaser.
- The questions are about plans and belong beside pricing — reach for the pricing-plus-FAQ band.
- The answers are really features carrying a creative panel — reach for an expandable feature /
  value-prop role.

## Character

Candid and thorough: a substantial, expandable list built for scanning, where the reader opens the one
answer they need and skips the rest. It is the family's most capacious shape — organised so length never
costs findability — and it stays honest and anticipatory, meeting each doubt at the moment it forms.

## Composition

It sits late, at the last doubt-clearing beat before the close, as a long expandable list kept scannable
by its split arrangement. Every entry must be a real question the reader would ask, findable on its own;
the list must stay searchable at length, and it should not sprawl past the doubts that genuinely stand
between the reader and acting.

## Contract

Assumes a genuine set of the reader's remaining questions with honest answers, including acknowledged
limits. It expects real questions and searchable structure; marketing copy dressed as a question betrays
it, and a long list the reader cannot scan defeats the very reason this heavier shape exists.

## Judgement

Used well, a reader with several "but what about…" doubts finds and opens exactly the ones they hold,
and none becomes a silent exit. The tempting misuse is padding the list with promotional
pseudo-questions, or letting it grow so long it can no longer be scanned. Its whole value is answering
real objections findably — break the honesty or the scannability and it stops clearing doubt.
