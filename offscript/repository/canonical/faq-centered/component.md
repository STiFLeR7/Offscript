# faq-centered

Short, quiet lists near the page end.

## Purpose

The centered FAQ: a short, quiet expandable list of the few remaining questions, set in a single
centered column near the end of the page. It handles the light case — when only a handful of real doubts
remain — clearing them calmly just before the close without the weight of a long, structured list.

## Choose when

- A small number of known objections remain between understanding and action.
- The list is short and quiet, and a calm centered column suits the moment.
- It belongs near the page end, as a gentle last doubt-clearing beat before the close.
- The few questions are genuine and can be answered plainly.

## Avoid when

- The list is long and must stay scannable — reach for the split column.
- The presentation should be as bare as possible — reach for the minimal single column.
- Only a teaser is wanted with answers elsewhere — reach for the chip teaser.
- The answers are really features carrying a creative panel — reach for an expandable feature /
  value-prop role.

## Character

Calm and closing-adjacent: a short, centered, expandable list read quietly near the end. Lighter than
the split shape, it keeps the family's candour — real questions, honest answers, opened on demand — but
at a hush, resolving a few last doubts without demanding the attention a long structured list would.

## Composition

It sits near the page end, a centered single column of a few expandable entries, just before the close.
The questions must be real and few; it should stay short and quiet rather than swelling toward a long
list, and each answer should meet its doubt plainly without turning into a pitch.

## Contract

Assumes a small set of genuine remaining questions with honest, plain answers. It expects the list to
stay short and real; stretched into a long list it loses the calm that justifies the centered form, and
filled with marketing pseudo-questions it stops clearing anything.

## Judgement

Used well, a nearly-convinced reader clears their last few doubts quietly and moves into the close. The
tempting misuse is loading a centered column past the point where it stays quick, or reaching for it
when the list is really long and needs the split shape's structure. Its value is a calm, honest
finish — keep it short, keep it real.
