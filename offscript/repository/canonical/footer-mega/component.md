# footer-mega

Editorial/brand-forward mega-footer.

## Purpose

The brand-led close: the family's most expansive and expressive footer, where the bottom of the page
is given room to carry the brand's voice alongside its wayfinding rather than settling for a plain
utility row. It exists when the close is a deliberate part of the identity — a considered final
impression — so a reader who reaches the end meets both a complete map onward and the site's character
one last time.

## Choose when

- The site is brand-led and its ending deserves editorial weight, not just a link table.
- The close should leave a considered final impression rather than trail off into fine print.
- There is genuine breadth to organize — many destinations, a brand statement, a full utility row.
- The page can carry an expressive, generous footer without it competing with the primary ask above.

## Avoid when

- The page just needs bare, composable link columns — reach for the unadorned link set.
- A short page wants its close folded into a single action band — reach for the CTA-fused footer.
- The ending's real job is a final capture — reach for the footer with a built-in form.
- Orientation is needed at the top of the page, not the bottom — that is the navigation role.

## Character

Expansive and brand-forward: the family's fullest voice, trading the plain utility row for an editorial
close that still reads as quiet against the body above. It is scanned for a destination like any footer,
but rewards a lingering reader with the brand's character — generous in space, considered in tone, never
loud enough to rival the page's primary ask.

## Composition

It is the closing band of a brand-led page, composing multi-column destination links, a brand mark or
statement, and the low-key legal and utility rows into one generous whole. Its structure must stay
consistent with the footer on every other page so the learned map holds; the extra room is for
expressiveness and completeness, not for a second competing call to action.

## Contract

Assumes a brand that wants its close to carry identity and a page that has earned a generous ending. It
expects the breadth to be real — genuine destinations, an honest brand voice — and the structure to
match the site's other footers exactly; a mega-footer that drifts page to page, or fills its room with
decoration over navigation, breaks the orientation frame it exists to complete.

## Judgement

Used well, a reader arrives at the bottom of a brand-led page and finds both a complete way onward and a
last, characterful impression of who they are dealing with. The tempting misuse is spectacle — filling
the space with brand flourish until the wayfinding is buried, or letting a second loud ask crowd the
primary one. Its value is a generous, on-brand close that still does the footer's quiet job of pointing
the way.
