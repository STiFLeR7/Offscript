# stat-band

Quiet, low-drama proof between calmer bands.

## Purpose

Slips a row of supporting figures into the page quietly, proving a point without raising its voice.
It solves the over-proof problem: when a page already carries a loud proof moment, a second one
would tire the reader — so this band states its numbers plainly, as a low-drama beat that
reinforces the argument while letting the surrounding sections breathe.

## Choose when

- A point benefits from a few numbers, but the moment calls for restraint rather than a punch.
- The page already has its loud proof beat and needs supporting evidence that does not compete.
- The figures should register in passing, between calmer bands, without demanding a pause.
- An understated, even row reads as more credible here than a dramatic treatment.

## Avoid when

- This is the page's single most important proof and it deserves emphasis — reach for a strong
  contrasting proof beat instead.
- There are no real numbers to state — a quiet row of weak figures still adds nothing.
- The argument is qualitative — reach for a descriptive band or a human-voice proof.

## Character

A calm, low-drama band of modest density and even, unforced energy: figures stated in an inline
row that supports rather than interrupts. It is the quiet member of the proof family, read in
passing and deliberately holding its volume down.

## Composition

It sits between calmer bands as supporting proof, often after the louder evidence has already
landed, and hands off without a jolt. It works precisely because it does not compete — placed
beside the page's heavy proof beat, it either disappears or muddies the emphasis.

## Contract

Assumes a few real, current, verifiable numbers worth stating plainly, with no need for dramatic
framing. It expects honest figures stated specifically — its credibility comes from restraint, not
from volume.

## Judgement

Used well, supporting numbers register quietly and reinforce the case without stealing attention
from the main proof. The tempting misuse is asking this quiet row to carry the page's headline
evidence, where its restraint reads as indifference. When the page's strongest proof is delivered
in a whisper, the figures land softer than they deserve.
