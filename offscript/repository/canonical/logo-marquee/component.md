# logo-marquee

A trust beat within a contrasting rhythm.

## Purpose

A continuous, glanceable band of trust marks placed as a beat within the page's contrasting rhythm. It
carries the same borrowed-credibility signal as the early strip, but earns its place deeper in the
flow — a moment of ambient reassurance set off from the bands around it, often doubling as a partner or
integration roll where breadth of company is itself the point.

## Choose when

- The page has a rhythm of alternating bands and a trust beat belongs partway through it, not only at
  the top.
- Many marks are worth showing together and their sheer breadth reads as momentum.
- A partner or integration ecosystem is part of the credibility, and the roll of names says "we sit
  among these."
- A contrasting moment is wanted to reset the reader between heavier arguments.

## Avoid when

- The trust marks belong at the very start, before any reasons — reach for the early strip under the
  hero.
- Only a few marks exist; a flowing band of a handful reads as thin, not broad.
- An about page wants a deliberate, assembled wall of backers — reach for that shape.
- The proof is a voice — reach for a testimonials role — or a number — reach for a stats / outcomes
  role.

## Character

Ambient and rhythmic: a wide, flowing run of marks that reads as continuous breadth rather than a fixed
list. It is a punctuation beat, not a destination — busy in the sense that many names pass the eye, calm
in that none demands a stop. It signals; it never asserts or invites action.

## Composition

It sits partway down the page, set on a contrasting surface so it reads as a distinct beat between
adjacent bands, and passes the reader on without asking anything. It should offer genuine breadth, never
recycle a thin set to fake volume, and never become the loudest thing on the page.

## Contract

Assumes many real, permitted marks — enough that breadth is the honest impression. It expects every
name to be genuine and every partnership real; a padded run, a recycled handful, or an implied
relationship that does not exist collapses the breadth it trades on.

## Judgement

Used well, a reader mid-page is reassured by the sheer company this keeps and moves on with more trust
than they had. The misuse is manufacturing breadth from too few marks, or letting a lively band pull
focus from the argument it is meant to support. It warms trust in passing; it never converts, and never
earns a place as the page's centre.
