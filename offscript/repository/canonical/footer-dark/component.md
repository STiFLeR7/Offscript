# footer-dark

Footer with a capture form built in.

## Purpose

The footer with capture: a link-rich close that carries a final, low-key capture — a newsletter or
email sign-up — built into the wayfinding itself. It exists for the reader who reaches the bottom still
mildly interested but not ready to commit: it offers a small, standing way to stay in touch alongside
the map onward, turning the end of the page into a soft opt-in rather than a hard ask or a dead end.

## Choose when

- A standing newsletter or email capture belongs at the close, beside full wayfinding.
- The reader who reaches the bottom may want to stay in touch without a larger commitment.
- The footer should offer a quiet, ongoing opt-in rather than the page's primary conversion.
- Complete link columns and a modest capture can coexist without either crowding the other.

## Avoid when

- The page needs bare link columns with no ask at all — reach for the plain link set.
- The capture is the page's central, high-intent conversion — that belongs to a contact or lead role.
- A short page wants a single fused action band — reach for the CTA-fused footer.
- Orientation is needed at the top of the page, not the bottom — that is the navigation role.

## Character

Complete and quietly acquisitive: a full footer that also keeps a small door open. It stays wayfinding
first — the capture is one modest field among the link columns, not the band's headline — and asks
softly, offering to stay in touch rather than pressing for the primary action. Its ask is standing and
low-key, never the loud conversion the body above already made.

## Composition

It combines the footer's multi-column destination links, brand mark, and utility row with a small,
built-in capture field. The capture must read as subordinate to the wayfinding and to the page's primary
ask; the links must stay complete despite it. Its structure — links and the standing capture alike —
must match the footer on every other page so the reader's map holds.

## Contract

Assumes a genuine reason to offer an ongoing opt-in and a capture modest enough to sit inside a footer.
It expects the ask to stay small and honest — a real way to stay in touch, not a high-pressure grab — and
the wayfinding to remain first; a footer whose capture swells into the primary conversion, or crowds out
the links, inverts the family's wayfinding purpose.

## Judgement

Used well, a mildly-interested reader at the bottom finds both a complete way onward and a small,
standing way to stay in touch, with no pressure. The tempting misuse is letting the capture dominate —
turning the closing frame into a lead-gen band — or bolting a form onto a page that only needed links.
Its value is a quiet, ongoing opt-in that rides alongside the footer's real job of pointing the way.
