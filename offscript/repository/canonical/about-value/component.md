# about-value

About-page value band; pairs media with numbers.

## Purpose

Carries a value argument in an about-page register, pairing a media moment with real numbers so the
organisation's story arrives with proof. It solves the narrative-without-substance problem: on an
about page, where a brand wants to be understood rather than sold to, this band keeps the telling
human while grounding it in evidence.

## Choose when

- The context is an about or company page, not a hard-conversion landing.
- The story benefits from a media moment paired with real, supporting numbers.
- The reader wants to understand who this is and see that the claims are backed.
- A considered, editorial value band fits the page's calmer intent.

## Avoid when

- The page is a conversion-focused landing — reach for a tighter value or feature band that moves
  faster.
- There is media but no real numbers to anchor it — pretty visuals without proof read as
  decoration.
- The point is purely the team — reach for a team / about band rather than a value treatment.

## Character

An editorial, considered band of medium density and calmer energy: media paired with proof on a
restful surface. The intent is to be absorbed thoughtfully rather than scanned quickly, balancing a
narrative moment with the numbers that steady it.

## Composition

It sits within an about or company page, mid-flow, bridging narrative and proof and sitting
comfortably near team material. It is out of place on a fast landing page, where its considered
pace slows the conversion the page is built for.

## Contract

Assumes an about-page context with both a genuine media asset and real supporting numbers. It
expects the two to reinforce each other — the media sets the tone, the figures make it credible —
not media standing in for substance.

## Judgement

Used well, an about page tells its story with a media moment and proves it with numbers in the same
breath. The tempting misuse is reaching for it on a hard-sell landing, or pairing handsome media
with empty proof. When the figures are missing or hollow, the media reads as decoration and the
band says nothing the reader can trust.
