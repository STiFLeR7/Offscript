# contact-us

Contact plus newsletter combo.

## Purpose

The contact-plus-newsletter combo: a contact capture paired with a newsletter sign-up, for the moment
when both opening a conversation and inviting an ongoing subscription are genuinely wanted. It lets a
reader either start a direct exchange or opt into staying in touch, without forcing a single path, while
keeping each ask light.

## Choose when

- Both a direct contact and a newsletter subscription are genuinely useful next steps.
- A reader might not be ready to talk but would opt into staying in touch — or the reverse.
- Offering both, clearly separated, serves the reader better than a single form.
- The moment supports two low-cost doors rather than one.

## Avoid when

- Only general contact is wanted — reach for the plain capture-plus-reassurance split.
- A newsletter alone is the ask, with no contact — that is a different, lighter capture.
- The page is support-style needing contact methods — reach for the methods-plus-form layout.
- The next step is a single freely-chosen action, not information — reach for a call-to-action role.

## Character

Low-friction and dual-doored: two related invitations — talk to us, or stay in touch — presented so
neither crowds the other. It keeps the family's reassuring restraint on both asks, and stays honest that
they are distinct choices, opening two doors rather than disguising one as the other.

## Composition

It sits where both a conversation and a subscription are wanted, pairing a contact capture with a clearly
separate newsletter sign-up. Each must ask only for what it needs, the two must not blur into one
confusing form, and both must be honest about what happens next — a newsletter opt-in is not a contact
request, and neither should be smuggled in under the other.

## Contract

Assumes both asks are genuinely wanted and each is kept minimal and distinct. It expects the two doors to
be honest and separate — never harvesting a newsletter address under the guise of contact, never
pre-checking a subscription; conflating them, or over-asking on either, breaks the low-friction trust the
family requires.

## Judgement

Used well, a reader picks the door that fits — start a conversation, or simply stay in touch — at low
cost either way. The tempting misuse is blurring the two so a reader subscribes without meaning to, or
piling fields onto both. Its value is two honest, separate invitations — keep them distinct and light,
or the combo becomes a trap rather than a convenience.
