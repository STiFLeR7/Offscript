# hero-lending

Calm credibility hero; cards-of-proof beneath the promise.

## Purpose

The opening screen for a product that wins trust by composure rather than spectacle. It answers the
credibility problem for an audience wary of loud claims: it states one promise plainly and settles a
small, orderly rank of proof cards beneath it, letting quiet evidence reassure instead of a busy
cluster.

## Choose when

- Credibility is the first thing to establish, and it is best conveyed calmly — a considered brand, a
  regulated or high-trust context, a discerning reader.
- A few supporting proofs read better arranged beneath the promise than packed around it.
- Early in the journey, opening a page whose argument will unfold steadily rather than shout.
- The audience is reassured by restraint and put off by a hard sell.

## Avoid when

- The strongest argument is a dense constellation of proof shown all at once — reach for a proof-tile
  opening, whose busy arrangement carries more weight.
- A genuine product artifact would say more than proof cards — reach for a product-state opening.
- The opening needs to invite interaction or capture intent — reach for a conversational opening.

## Character

Calm, measured, and confident — deliberately low in emotional energy, trading urgency for assurance.
The promise leads; the proof cards support it in a quiet, ordered rank beneath. It is read, not
operated, and its restraint is the message, not a shortfall.

## Composition

It opens the page, so little precedes it. It hands off gracefully to a feature or how-it-works
section that expands the promise the cards vouched for, and later into deeper social proof or the
partners the calm opening set up. It should not sit beside another hero, nor be crowded immediately
by a loud, high-energy band that breaks the composure it establishes.

## Contract

Assumes a handful of genuine, quietly persuasive proofs exist — real trust marks, honest cards, or
credible partners — to sit beneath the promise. It expects one clear promise, one primary action, and
a small set of true supporting proofs; padded with filler cards, the calm reads as empty rather than
assured.

## Judgement

Used well, a trustworthy product opens with a plain promise and a few honest proofs settled beneath
it, reassuring without raising its voice. The tempting misuse is filling the card rank with weak or
vanity proof to look substantial, or choosing calm when the moment actually calls for a confident,
proof-dense opening. When the supporting cards are hollow the composure reads as thinness and the
promise loses its footing; over-filled, the calm it depends on collapses into clutter.
