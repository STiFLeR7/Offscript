# customer-story

Deep single story plus outcomes.

## Purpose

The deep single-story shape: one customer's journey told at length, end to end, with the outcomes it
produced. It answers the need for narrative proof — when the most convincing evidence is a complete arc
from problem to result for a single, named customer, told with enough depth that the reader can see
themselves in it.

## Choose when

- One customer's full arc — situation, decision, outcome — is the most persuasive proof available.
- The story rewards depth: the detail of how it unfolded is what convinces.
- Near a considered decision, where a reader wants one relatable journey rather than many fragments.
- Real outcomes exist to anchor the narrative's end.

## Avoid when

- Breadth across many customers matters more than one deep arc — reach for a multi-voice set.
- Several structured cases browsed together fit better — reach for an editorial case-study band.
- A compact quote-plus-metrics card already carries the point — reach for that lighter shape.
- The proof is a mark — reach for a social-proof role — or a number alone — reach for a stats /
  outcomes role.

## Character

Immersive and narrative, high in depth and low in count: a single story given real length, paired with
the outcomes that resolve it. It is read, not operated — a sustained account meant to be followed
rather than scanned, trading breadth for the pull of one complete journey.

## Composition

It sits in the body where the page can afford a longer proof passage, beside or after the claims the
story substantiates, and leads naturally toward outcomes or the ask. It should not sit beside another
deep testimonials band, nor be compressed so far that the narrative loses the depth that justifies it.

## Contract

Assumes one real, permitted customer whose full story — problem, journey, and result — can be told
truthfully, with genuine outcomes. It expects the narrative and its results to be real and specific; a
composited or embellished journey collapses the proof, and a story without a concrete outcome ends
without paying off.

## Judgement

Used well, one named customer's complete journey shows a reader exactly how this delivered for someone
like them. The tempting misuse is fictionalising or compositing the arc, or choosing a long story when
breadth or a quick card would persuade more. Invented, it is the family's most damaging failure;
stripped of real outcomes, the narrative builds to nothing.
