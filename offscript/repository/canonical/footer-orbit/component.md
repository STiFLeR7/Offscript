# footer-orbit

Decorated footer; rounds off a quiet preceding band.

## Purpose

The decorated round-off: a footer whose visual finish is its reason for being — it closes a calm,
quiet preceding band with a considered flourish so the page ends on a deliberate note rather than
trailing off. It exists to resolve a subdued ending: where a plain footer under a quiet band would feel
like the page simply stopped, this rounds the close with decoration, giving a soft ending its full stop.

## Choose when

- The band just above is quiet or subdued and the page would otherwise seem to trail off.
- The close deserves a considered visual finish to resolve a soft ending on a deliberate note.
- A little decoration at the very bottom would feel like completion rather than noise.
- The footer can carry finish and wayfinding together without the ornament overwhelming the links.

## Avoid when

- The preceding band is already loud or commanding — a decorated round-off would gild it.
- The page just needs bare, composable link columns — reach for the plain link set.
- The ending's real work is a final action or capture — reach for the CTA-fused or capture footer.
- Orientation is needed at the top of the page, not the bottom — that is the navigation role.

## Character

Finished and resolving: the family's most decorated shape, using visual flourish to give a quiet ending
its close. The ornament serves resolution, not spectacle — it rounds off a soft band so the page ends
deliberately, while the wayfinding beneath stays complete and legible. It is a graceful full stop, never
a loud finale competing with the page's primary ask.

## Composition

It is a footer dressed with decoration — a rounding visual finish — over the usual destination links,
brand mark, and utility row. The ornament must resolve the quiet band above without burying the
wayfinding or drifting the footer's structure from the site's other pages; the links must stay complete
and consistent beneath whatever finish it wears.

## Contract

Assumes a genuinely quiet preceding band that benefits from a resolved close, and decoration restrained
enough to finish rather than shout. It expects the flourish to serve the ending — a deliberate full stop
— while the wayfinding stays first and consistent; ornament laid over an already-loud band, or thick
enough to obscure the links, betrays the gentle resolution this variant exists to give.

## Judgement

Used well, a soft, quiet band resolves into a footer that feels finished and intentional, closing the
page on a deliberate note instead of a fade-out. The tempting misuse is decorating for its own sake —
dressing a footer that needed no finish, or gilding a band that was already loud — until the ornament
competes rather than resolves. Its value is a graceful full stop for an ending that would otherwise
trail away.
