# faq-single

Minimal single column; no creative panel.

## Purpose

The minimal single-column FAQ: the barest form of the doubt-clearing list — a plain, expandable single
column with no creative panel beside it. It exists for the case where the questions alone should carry
the beat, stripped of any accompanying visual, so the reader's attention rests wholly on the answers to
their remaining doubts.

## Choose when

- A short set of real questions is best answered with nothing else competing for attention.
- The moment wants the plainest possible treatment — no creative panel, just questions and answers.
- The doubts are straightforward and a single unadorned column serves them cleanly.
- It sits late, as an understated doubt-clearing beat before the close.

## Avoid when

- The list is long and must stay scannable — reach for the split column.
- A creative panel genuinely belongs beside the answers — that is a different, richer treatment.
- The answers are really features carrying a creative panel — reach for an expandable feature /
  value-prop role.
- Only a pointer is needed with the full FAQ elsewhere — reach for the chip teaser.

## Character

Plain and undistracted: the family's most stripped shape — a single expandable column, no panel, nothing
beside the questions. It is quiet to the point of austerity, trusting the honesty and relevance of the
answers alone to clear doubt, and it stays candid and direct without any visual accompaniment.

## Composition

It sits late as a single unadorned column of expandable entries, deliberately without a creative panel.
The questions must be real and the column must stay the sole focus; it should not acquire decorative
company, and if a panel truly belongs, the moment wants a richer shape, not this one.

## Contract

Assumes genuine remaining questions with honest answers and no need for an accompanying visual. It
expects the bareness to be a fit, not a lack; dressing pseudo-questions as content still betrays it, and
adding the very panel this shape excludes means a different treatment was actually needed.

## Judgement

Used well, a reader meets a clean, plain set of real answers with nothing to distract from them, and
their doubts resolve. The tempting misuse is bolting on the creative panel this shape exists to omit, or
using austerity where a long list needed structure. Its value is undistracted candour — the plainness is
the point.
