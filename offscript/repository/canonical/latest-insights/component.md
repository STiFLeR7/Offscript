# latest-insights

Editorial article cards; the blog teaser.

## Purpose

The blog teaser: an editorial grid of the offering's own articles and insights, surfaced so a reader can
see there is real, current thinking behind the product and follow a path deeper if they want one. It is
the family's own-voice variant — the organization's published articles as evidence of a living,
substantive offering, offered rather than forced.

## Choose when

- The offering has genuine, current articles or insights of its own worth surfacing.
- Demonstrating ongoing thought and freshness will strengthen the case at this point in the page.
- A curious reader should be given a path deeper without taxing everyone who does not want one.
- Editorial cards read better here than a press row or a denser catalogue grid.

## Avoid when

- There is no real, current editorial material — an empty or invented blog teaser is worse than none.
- The coverage to show is third-party press rather than the offering's own writing — reach for the news row.
- More items must fit than an editorial grid holds comfortably — reach for the denser grid or the carousel.
- The section must carry the primary conversion itself — this supports the decision, it does not ask for it.

## Character

Substantive and current, in the offering's own voice: a calm editorial grid that shows there is real
thinking behind the product. It is scanned for relevance and lets the reader self-select an article to
follow; it offers depth, never forces it, and stays a supporting surface that never depends on the deeper
layer to make sense on its own.

## Composition

It is a grid of editorial article cards — the offering's own insights — each a real, current item with a
link that resolves. The surface must read completely on its own for a reader who follows nothing, while
giving the curious a genuine path onward; every card must be real writing, never a fabricated title or
an invented post.

## Contract

Assumes the offering actually publishes current thinking and that every card is a real article with a
working link. It expects freshness and honesty as hard conditions — no fabricated posts, no dead links —
because the variant's whole value is being evidence of a living offering; a teaser of invented or stale
articles undermines the trust it exists to build.

## Judgement

Used well, a reader gauges that the offering is alive and substantive and can follow a genuine article
deeper before deciding. The tempting misuse is dressing the grid with fabricated or invented posts to
look active, or linking to pieces that do not resolve — which reads as hollow the moment it is tested.
Its value is honest evidence of ongoing substance, in the offering's own voice.
