# integrations

Lighter marquee; the marks speak alone.

## Purpose

The lighter integration marquee: a quiet, flowing band of connection marks shown without per-connection
copy, for when the marks speak for themselves. It answers "does this work with what I already have?" at a
glance — the reader scans for their own tools among recognisable names, reassured of fit without needing
each connection spelled out.

## Choose when

- Ecosystem fit is part of the decision but the connection marks are recognisable on their own.
- A quieter, lighter beat is wanted — a passing reassurance, not a considered grid.
- The reader only needs to spot their tools among the marks, not read about each one.
- Breadth of recognisable connections carries the message without explanation.

## Avoid when

- Each connection needs a line of explanation to make its fit clear — reach for the grid.
- The marks are obscure enough that they argue nothing without copy.
- The marks signal trust rather than compatibility — reach for a social-proof role.
- The fit implied is not real — a marquee of unsupported marks is decoration that misleads.

## Character

Practical and light: a quiet, flowing band of recognisable connection marks, scanned rather than
studied. It is the lighter of the two integration shapes — no per-mark copy, just the marks doing the
arguing — and stays a reassuring passing beat, signalling fit without asserting or asking anything.

## Composition

It sits mid-page as a light band of connection marks, without the per-connection copy the grid carries.
The marks must be recognisable enough to argue fit on their own; it should offer genuine breadth rather
than a padded few, and stay a quiet beat that clears an adoption doubt without becoming a focal feature.

## Contract

Assumes real, supported connections whose marks are recognisable enough to speak without copy. It
expects every mark to be a genuine, existing integration — never implying a fit that is not real; marks
the reader cannot recognise argue nothing here, and a band of aspirational or unsupported connections is
misleading decoration.

## Judgement

Used well, a reader glances across the band, spots the tools they already use, and stops worrying that
adopting this means abandoning them. The tempting misuse is a marquee of marks too obscure to argue
anything, or implying integrations that do not exist. Its value is quick, recognisable reassurance — the
marks must be real and known, or the lighter shape says nothing.
