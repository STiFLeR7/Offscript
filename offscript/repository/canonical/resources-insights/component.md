# resources-insights

Denser resources grid.

## Purpose

The denser catalogue grid: the family's higher-capacity layout, for when there is more real material to
surface than an editorial teaser holds comfortably. It shows the offering is alive and substantive by
presenting a fuller set of resources at once, letting a reader scan a broad shelf for relevance and
follow whatever fits — evidence of depth by breadth.

## Choose when

- There is a genuinely larger body of real, current resources than a lighter teaser can show.
- Breadth itself makes the case — a fuller shelf signals a substantive, well-supported offering.
- A reader benefits from seeing more options at once and self-selecting from among them.
- A static, higher-density grid serves better here than a sliding carousel or a sparse teaser.

## Avoid when

- Only a few editorial pieces are worth showing — reach for the lighter blog teaser.
- The item count exceeds what any static grid holds well — reach for the carousel instead.
- The material is third-party press rather than the offering's resources — reach for the news row.
- Density would crowd the page or bury the primary ask — a fuller grid must still earn its room.

## Character

Substantive through breadth: the family's fullest static surface, quietly comprehensive rather than
loud. It packs more real items into one scan without becoming a wall, still offering paths rather than
forcing them, and — like every family member — reads completely on its own for a reader who follows none
of its links.

## Composition

It is a denser grid of resource cards than the editorial teaser, each a real, current item with a
resolving link, arranged so the extra density stays scannable rather than overwhelming. Every card must
be genuine material; the added capacity is for real breadth, never padding, and the surface must never
depend on the deeper layer to make sense.

## Contract

Assumes enough real, current resources to justify the higher density and a reader who benefits from
seeing them together. It expects every one of the many cards to be genuine with a working link — the more
items shown, the more places a fabricated item or dead link can betray the offering; breadth built from
invented items inverts the substance it is meant to prove.

## Judgement

Used well, a reader takes in a broad, credible shelf of real resources and finds exactly the one that
fits, coming away confident the offering is deep and well-supported. The tempting misuse is inflating the
grid with filler to look comprehensive, which a reader detects as soon as a card leads nowhere. Its value
is honest breadth: density that is all real substance.
