# contact-form

Plain capture plus reassurance.

## Purpose

The plain capture split: a labelled field set with a submit action and reassurance beside it, for
general contact. It is the family's canonical shape — the lowest-friction way to open a conversation,
asking only for what is needed and placing the proof or risk-reduction that earns the submission right
next to the fields.

## Choose when

- A ready reader's next step is to make general contact, and a simple form is the right door.
- Only a few fields are genuinely needed, and reassurance can sit beside them.
- The task is plain contact, not a support desk, a demo booking, or a dedicated page.
- The moment is one of readiness — the reader wants a low-cost way to start.

## Avoid when

- Both contact and a newsletter sign-up are wanted — reach for the contact-plus-newsletter combo.
- The page is support-style and needs contact methods beside the form — reach for the methods-plus-form
  layout.
- The next step is a single freely-chosen action, not handing over information — reach for a
  call-to-action role.
- A whole dedicated contact page is called for — reach for the full standalone body.

## Character

Low-friction and reassuring: a compact form that opens a door rather than guarding one. It keeps the
family's discipline visible — only the fields that are needed, reassurance at their side — and stays
plain and calm, weighing the reader's effort at every field rather than demanding it.

## Composition

It sits where the next step is to hand over information, as a split of a short field set and the
reassurance that supports it. Every field must earn its place; the reassurance must sit with the form,
not pages away; and it must be honest about what happens after submitting rather than demanding the
action before trust exists.

## Contract

Assumes a ready reader and only the fields the task truly needs, with reassurance beside them. It expects
restraint — each extra field raises the cost of yes — and honesty about what follows; a form that
over-asks, hides what happens next, or demands the action before trust has been earned raises the very
cost the family exists to lower.

## Judgement

Used well, a ready reader hands over just enough to start the conversation, at the lowest cost the task
allows. The tempting misuse is padding the form with fields the moment does not need, or asking before
the page has earned trust. Its value is a low, honest door — keep the fields few and the reassurance
close, or every extra field costs a conversation.
