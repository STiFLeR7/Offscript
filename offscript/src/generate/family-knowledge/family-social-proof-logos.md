# Social proof / logos

The ambient-trust role: signalling that credible others already trust this.

## Purpose
Signal quickly and without argument that credible others already trust this.

## Mission
Lend borrowed credibility early, before the reader has been given reasons — durable across any rebrand.

## Information Density
Light: a row or wall of marks, optionally a quiet headline or a single anchoring figure.

## Reading Behaviour
Glanced, not read; recognition does the work in an instant.

## Interaction Style
Static and ambient; it signals, it does not assert or invite action.

## Decision Style
Chosen when borrowed credibility belongs early; an early trust strip of marks, a credibility wall on about pages, or a proof hybrid when one story should anchor a few numbers.

## Strengths
Buys continued attention with a low-effort "serious people already rely on this."

## Weaknesses
It is support, not the argument; it fails the moment a mark is fabricated or a relationship implied that does not exist.

## Avoid When
When the proof is a human voice (reach for a testimonials role) or a number (reach for a stats/outcomes role).

## Typical Inputs
Real, permitted client/partner/investor marks; optionally one anchoring quote or metric.

## Typical Outputs
A reader given an immediate, low-effort reason to keep reading.

## Sibling Differences
An early trust strip of marks only; an investor or credibility wall for about pages; a proof hybrid when one customer story should anchor a few numbers.

## Escalation Rules
Marks only, no claims; every name, mark, and figure real and permitted — never fabricate or imply a relationship that does not exist; ambient trust, never the page's focal point.

## Family Character
Quiet and ambient — credibility by association, never the loudest beat.

## Conversion Style
Warms trust early so later asks land; it never converts directly.

## Expected Visitor State
An early reader, not yet given reasons, deciding whether to invest more attention.
