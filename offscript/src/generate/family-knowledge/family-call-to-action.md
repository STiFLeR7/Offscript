# Call-to-action

The conversion role: turning earned belief into a single, freely-chosen next step.

## Purpose
Convert earned understanding and trust into a single, freely-chosen next step.

## Mission
Offer the reader one obvious, low-cost next step at the moment of peak readiness — durable across any rebrand.

## Information Density
Spare and focused: a promise, optional supporting copy, and one primary action.

## Reading Behaviour
Read quickly at the point of decision; it resolves rather than explains.

## Interaction Style
One primary action (or a capture form); competing asks are removed.

## Decision Style
Chosen at peak readiness as the page resolves; a short banner when a footer follows, a band with link columns for a near-footer hand-off, a decorated close for a marquee page, a softer close mid-page.

## Strengths
Turns earned belief into action by making the single next step obvious and low-cost.

## Weaknesses
Spends trust when it nags, repeats an unearned ask, or competes with a second primary action.

## Avoid When
When the next step is to hand over information — reach for a contact/lead-form role instead.

## Typical Inputs
A reader-framed promise and exactly one primary action (or a low-cost capture).

## Typical Outputs
A reader who takes the one obvious next step, freely chosen.

## Sibling Differences
The shortest banner when a footer follows immediately; a band with link columns for a near-footer hand-off; a decorated flagship close for a marquee page (once only); a softer mid-page close when a gentle invitation fits between bands.

## Escalation Rules
One primary action; placed where readiness peaks, never before; framed as the reader's gain; never nags or uses urgency tricks; decorated closing shapes at most once per page; one focal point.

## Family Character
Resolute and generous — a beginning offered to the reader, not a demand.

## Conversion Style
This is the conversion beat — it asks, once, at the moment readiness peaks, framed as the reader's gain.

## Expected Visitor State
A reader who has understood and believes, and now needs the one obvious thing to do next.
