# Contact / lead form

The capture role: opening a conversation at the lowest possible friction.

## Purpose
Capture intent or open a conversation while keeping the perceived cost of doing so as low as possible.

## Mission
Give a ready reader a low-friction way to start the conversation — a responsibility durable across any rebrand.

## Information Density
As light as the task allows: only the fields that are needed, with reassurance beside them.

## Reading Behaviour
Skimmed for effort then completed; the reader weighs the cost of the fields.

## Interaction Style
An input task — a labelled field set with a submit, sometimes as an overlay on another action.

## Decision Style
Chosen when the action is to make contact, book, or subscribe; a plain capture split for general contact, a contact-plus-newsletter combo when both are wanted, a methods-plus-form layout for support, a sales-led booking band for demos, an overlay attached to an action elsewhere, or a full standalone body for a dedicated page.

## Strengths
Converts readiness into a started conversation at the lowest friction the task permits.

## Weaknesses
Raises the cost of "yes" with every extra field, and fails when it demands the action before trust exists.

## Avoid When
When the next step is a single freely-chosen action rather than handing over information — reach for a call-to-action role instead.

## Typical Inputs
Only the necessary fields, a submit action, and reassurance (copy, contact methods, or proof) beside the form.

## Typical Outputs
A started conversation or captured intent, at low perceived cost.

## Sibling Differences
A plain capture-plus-reassurance split for general contact; a contact-plus-newsletter combo when both are wanted; a methods-plus-form layout for support pages; a sales-led booking band for demos; an overlay attached to an action elsewhere; a full standalone body for a dedicated contact page.

## Escalation Rules
Ask only for what is needed — every extra field raises the cost of yes; reassurance sits with the form; honest about what happens next, never demand the action before understanding and trust exist.

## Family Character
Low-friction and reassuring — it opens a door rather than guarding one.

## Conversion Style
Converts by lowering the cost of handing over information at the moment of readiness.

## Expected Visitor State
A ready reader whose next step is to make contact, book, or subscribe.
