# Footer

The closing frame: complete, low-effort wayfinding for a reader at the bottom.

## Purpose
Close the page and provide complete, low-effort wayfinding for a reader who has reached the end or scrolled past everything.

## Mission
Give a reader at the bottom an easy way onward rather than a dead end — durable across any rebrand.

## Information Density
Comprehensive but quiet: multi-column link sets, a brand mark, and low-key legal/utility rows.

## Reading Behaviour
Scanned for a destination; read only to find the way onward.

## Interaction Style
Wayfinding links, optionally a final low-key action or capture; never a competing primary ask.

## Decision Style
The closing band of essentially every page; a link-rich footer with capture when a final newsletter ask fits, a decorated footer to round off a quiet band, an editorial mega-footer for a brand-led site, a CTA-fused footer on short pages, or a bare link set when composing.

## Strengths
Turns the end of the page into an onward path, complete enough to navigate from with no prior context.

## Weaknesses
Breaks the learned map if its structure drifts page to page, or dilutes the page if it competes with the primary ask.

## Avoid When
When orientation is needed at the top of the page rather than at the close — that is the navigation role.

## Typical Inputs
The site's destination links, a brand mark, an optional final action or capture, and legal/utility rows.

## Typical Outputs
A reader who reached the bottom and has an easy way onward.

## Sibling Differences
A link-rich footer with capture built in; a decorated footer to round off a quiet preceding band; an editorial brand-forward mega-footer for a brand-led site; a CTA-fused footer for a one-band close on short pages; a bare link set for composing.

## Escalation Rules
Consistent across the whole site — its structure must not drift; orients a reader who scrolled past everything, complete with no prior context; may carry a final action, but never one that competes with the page's single primary ask.

## Family Character
Steady and complete — the page's close and the bottom half of its orientation frame.

## Conversion Style
May carry a final, subordinate action, but its job is wayfinding, not the primary ask.

## Expected Visitor State
A reader who has reached the end or scrolled past everything and needs a way onward.
