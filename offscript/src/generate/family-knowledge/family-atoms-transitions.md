# Atoms & transitions

The parts-and-punctuation role: shared building blocks and deliberate hand-offs between bands.

## Purpose
Supply the shared, reusable parts other components are built from, and mark deliberate hand-offs between bands.

## Mission
Provide the canonical building blocks and the punctuation between sections — a responsibility durable across any rebrand.

## Information Density
Minimal by definition: single molecules and punctuation, never a full band of content.

## Reading Behaviour
Noticed in passing, not read as a section; they support or separate, they do not communicate a whole idea.

## Interaction Style
Reusable parts — a canonical action, a chip rail, a status mock — and transition punctuation between bands.

## Decision Style
Reached for when assembling or punctuating, not when communicating a whole idea; the specific atom follows the part needed, and a divider only when a deliberate hand-off earns one.

## Strengths
Give the system one canonical source for shared parts and a disciplined way to mark deliberate shifts between bands.

## Weaknesses
Become noise the moment an atom stands in for a band's argument or a divider marks a hand-off that is not there.

## Avoid When
When a whole idea must be communicated — an atom is a part, not a section; reach for the appropriate full component instead.

## Typical Inputs
A shared building block to compose (an action, a chip rail, a problem card, a status mock) or a genuine hand-off to mark.

## Typical Outputs
A composed component part, or a legible transition between two contrasting bands.

## Sibling Differences
The canonical action styling as the single source for every call-to-action; chips as a hero or feature garnish; a problem card to compose into a grid; a status mock as a live-number filler; a transition divider only when a deliberate hand-off into a contrasting band earns one.

## Escalation Rules
An atom never stands in for a full band's argument; transitions are punctuation used sparingly — a divider with no hand-off is noise; the action styling is canonical and singular; every atom must earn its place and mean something where it is placed.

## Family Character
Quiet and utilitarian — parts and punctuation, never the argument.

## Conversion Style
Supplies the canonical action used by other components; on its own it neither argues nor converts.

## Expected Visitor State
Position-independent — encountered wherever a component is assembled or two bands hand off.
