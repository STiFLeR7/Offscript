# Resources / insights / news

The substance role: showing the offering is alive, current, and worth going deeper on.

## Purpose
Show the offering is alive and substantive by surfacing real articles, insights, or press — and offer a deeper path to readers who want one.

## Mission
Demonstrate ongoing substance and freshness, and let interested readers go deeper — across any rebrand.

## Information Density
Moderate: a grid or carousel of item cards; a surface that never depends on the deeper layer.

## Reading Behaviour
Scanned for relevance; the reader self-selects an item to follow.

## Interaction Style
Static grid or a sliding carousel; it offers paths, it does not force them.

## Decision Style
Chosen when depth or freshness helps the case; an editorial grid as a blog teaser, a denser grid when more must fit, a press row for third-party coverage, a carousel when items exceed a static band.

## Strengths
Signals a living, substantive offering and gives the curious a path deeper without taxing everyone else.

## Weaknesses
Undermines trust if items are placeholder or invented, or if a link does not resolve.

## Avoid When
When there is no real, current material to show — an empty or invented version is worse than none.

## Typical Inputs
Real, current articles, insights, or press items, each with a resolving link.

## Typical Outputs
A reader able to gauge ongoing substance or go deeper before deciding.

## Sibling Differences
An editorial article grid as a blog teaser; a denser resources grid when more must fit; a press/news row for third-party coverage; a carousel when more items than a static band holds must be shown.

## Escalation Rules
Real, current items only — never placeholder titles or invented coverage, every link must resolve; it offers depth, never forces it; it must self-orient a reader who arrived there.

## Family Character
Substantive and current — evidence of a living offering.

## Conversion Style
Deepens confidence through demonstrated substance; it supports the decision rather than asking for it.

## Expected Visitor State
A reader gauging ongoing substance or wanting to go deeper before deciding.
