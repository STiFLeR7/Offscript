# Process / how-it-works

The feasibility role: showing the offering is workable by laying out how it happens, in order.

## Purpose
Show the offering is workable by laying out how it happens, in order.

## Mission
Make "I could actually do this" believable by revealing the path from start to result — a brand-invariant responsibility.

## Information Density
Measured: a small number of ordered steps, each substantial enough to reward dwelling, with supporting media.

## Reading Behaviour
Read in sequence, step by step; the reader follows the path rather than scanning.

## Interaction Style
Largely static, often a guided walk; each step opens an expectation the next step closes.

## Decision Style
Chosen only when the content is genuinely a causal sequence; a fuller walk for substantial steps, a lighter version when the process simply leads into the ask.

## Strengths
Converts doubt about feasibility into belief by making the route concrete and ordered.

## Weaknesses
Fails when order is imposed on parallel items, or when a step resolves everything and gives the reader a place to leave.

## Avoid When
When the items are parallel rather than sequential — reach for a feature/value-prop role instead.

## Typical Inputs
A genuine ordered sequence of steps, supporting media, and an optional next action.

## Typical Outputs
A reader who believes the path is real and that they could follow it.

## Sibling Differences
A canonical multi-step walk for three-to-four substantial steps that reward dwelling; a lighter closing-half version when the process is a simple lead-in to the call to action.

## Escalation Rules
Only when the content truly is a sequence — never impose order on parallel items; each step opens onto the next; every step self-evident in order even to a reader who lands mid-sequence.

## Family Character
Methodical and reassuring — it demonstrates feasibility by ordered revelation.

## Conversion Style
Builds confidence that the offering is workable, handing a readier reader toward the ask.

## Expected Visitor State
A reader who understands what the offering is and now needs to believe it is achievable.
