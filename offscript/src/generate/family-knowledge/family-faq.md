# FAQ

The doubt-clearing role: answering the reader's last questions before the close.

## Purpose
Anticipate the reader's real remaining doubts and answer them at the moment they form, so an unresolved question never becomes a silent exit.

## Mission
Clear the last "but what about…" before the close — a responsibility that survives any rebrand.

## Information Density
Compact and scannable: an expandable question-and-answer list the reader can search.

## Reading Behaviour
Scanned for the reader's own question, then read; the rest is skipped.

## Interaction Style
Expandable disclosure — the reader opens the answer they need.

## Decision Style
Chosen when known objections stand between understanding and action; a split column for long lists, a centered or single column for short quiet lists near the end, a pricing-plus-FAQ band for plan questions, a chip teaser linking to a fuller FAQ.

## Strengths
Builds credibility by meeting objections early instead of deflecting them, removing silent exits.

## Weaknesses
Backfires when answers are marketing copy dressed as questions, or when a long list cannot be searched.

## Avoid When
When the answers are really features carrying a creative panel — reach for an expandable feature/value-prop role instead.

## Typical Inputs
The reader's actual remaining questions and honest answers, including acknowledged limits.

## Typical Outputs
A reader whose last doubt is resolved at the moment it formed.

## Sibling Differences
A split column for long lists that must stay scannable; a centered or single column for short, quiet lists near the page end; a pricing-plus-FAQ band when the questions are about plans; a chip teaser linking to a fuller FAQ elsewhere.

## Escalation Rules
Answers the reader's actual questions, never marketing dressed as a question; meets each doubt where the reader feels it; honest about limits; long lists stay scannable.

## Family Character
Candid and anticipatory — it answers the unspoken objection before it stops the reader.

## Conversion Style
Clears the final doubt so the close can land; it removes friction rather than asking.

## Expected Visitor State
A nearly-convinced reader with one remaining objection before acting.
