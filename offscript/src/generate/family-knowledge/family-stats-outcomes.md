# Stats / outcomes

The evidence role: proving the claim with specific, checkable numbers.

## Purpose
Prove the claim with specific, checkable numbers — the form of proof a skeptical reader extends the most belief to.

## Mission
Answer doubt with evidence the reader can check — how much, how many, how fast — across any rebrand.

## Information Density
Concentrated around a few large figures, with optional narrative; it needs rest around it to land.

## Reading Behaviour
Scanned for the headline number, then checked; the figures are read as evidence.

## Interaction Style
Static proof; occasionally a progress meter, but the numbers carry it.

## Decision Style
Chosen when a number, not a sentence, is the strongest argument; a strong contrasting beat for a mid-page punch, a quiet inline row for low-drama proof, an outcome band when numbers need narrative, drop-in tiles when composing.

## Strengths
Earns the most belief from a skeptical reader, because a precise figure is checkable in a way a sentence is not.

## Weaknesses
One inflated figure discredits the rest; placed in overload, the proof stops landing.

## Avoid When
When a human voice persuades more than a figure — reach for a testimonials role instead.

## Typical Inputs
Real, current, verifiable numbers, stated specifically, with optional narrative.

## Typical Outputs
A reader whose doubt is answered by evidence they can verify.

## Sibling Differences
A strong contrasting card beat for a mid-page punch; a quiet inline row for low-drama proof between calmer bands; an outcome band with meters when the numbers need narrative; drop-in tiles for composing metrics into another band.

## Escalation Rules
Every number real, current, and verifiable — never rounded up to impress; stated specifically; avoid numeric overload — never two heavy number bands adjacent; no claim without proof.

## Family Character
Confident and evidentiary — a contrasting beat where the numbers speak.

## Conversion Style
Converts skepticism into belief with checkable proof, strengthening the path to the ask.

## Expected Visitor State
A doubting reader who will believe evidence they can verify.
