# Testimonials / case studies

The lived-proof role: real customers vouching for the claim in a human voice.

## Purpose
Let real customers' words and results vouch for the claim, in a human voice the reader trusts.

## Mission
Have someone like the reader testify that this delivered — a responsibility durable across any rebrand.

## Information Density
Variable — from a single anchored quote to a multi-voice set or a deep narrative story.

## Reading Behaviour
Read for resonance; the reader looks for a voice like their own.

## Interaction Style
Mostly static; a one-at-a-time slider or carousel when many voices must be shown.

## Decision Style
Chosen when lived proof persuades more than the brand's own assertion; the shape follows whether one named story, breadth, a long single voice, volume, or narrative depth is needed.

## Strengths
Persuades where the brand's own assertion cannot — a credible, attributable human voice.

## Weaknesses
Collapses entirely if a quote, face, or result is fabricated or embellished; loses force when stranded far from the claim it supports.

## Avoid When
When the proof is a mark rather than a voice (reach for a social-proof role) or a number (reach for a stats/outcomes role).

## Typical Inputs
Real, permitted quotes with attribution and faces, optionally paired with real metrics or media.

## Typical Outputs
A reader who has heard from someone like them that this delivered.

## Sibling Differences
A single quote-plus-metrics card for one named customer; a multi-voice set to show breadth; a one-at-a-time slider for a long single voice; a review carousel for the volume-of-love signal; an editorial or deep single story when the proof needs narrative depth.

## Escalation Rules
Every quote, attribution, face, and result real and permitted — never fabricate, composite, or embellish; place the proof beside the claim or ask it supports; specificity persuades over vague praise.

## Family Character
Human and credible — lived proof in a trusted voice.

## Conversion Style
Converts doubt into trust at the point of decision by placing real voices beside the claim.

## Expected Visitor State
A reader near decision who needs to hear that this worked for someone like them.
